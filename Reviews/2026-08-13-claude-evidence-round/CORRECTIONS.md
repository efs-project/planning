## Citation hierarchy

1. Current owner rulings and owning decision inboxes establish project authority.
2. This README and correction register establish the round's scoped synthesis.
3. Curated reports provide methods, sources, and detailed findings.
4. Supporting reports provide raw breadth and counter-evidence.

Before using a dated external fact for deployment, legal clearance, security
policy, funding copy, or conference claims, refresh it from its cited primary
source. This corpus makes the original evidence recoverable; it does not make
point-in-time facts timeless.
