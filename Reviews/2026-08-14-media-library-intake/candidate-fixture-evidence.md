## Result

The smallest tagged-media case now has exact candidate image, derivative and
video bytes; two independently retained source observations for one blob;
screened source/rights evidence; plural authored claims; a separate synthetic
private relationship; an authored exact-duplicate claim; probabilistic
near-duplicate evidence; verified video chunks/ranges; and a corrupt-primary
fallback trace.

The public semantic packet keeps work, exact blob, representation, source
observation, authored edition and selection claim distinct. Author-neutral
statements are separate from fixture-local authored occurrences. No tested
obligation requires a media-specific contract, canonical tag authority,
moderation oracle, similarity oracle, ranking service or full-text index in
Core.

The candidate is still application evidence. It does not instantiate frozen
EFS Type/Record/Occurrence bytes, verified Principals, a Realm-qualified
current Binding, a Lens execution, signatures, admission, gas/state costs,
encryption, deployment or publication.

## Mature findings and remaining gates

### Mature evidence/recommendation

- exact blob identity is digest + byte length and never comes from a Locator,
  work, representation or probabilistic match;
- author-neutral statement content plus plural occurrences preserves agreement
  without duplicating semantic content and disagreement without canonical truth;
- source and rights observations survive exact deduplication and retain screened
  exact evidence bindings;
- private/local organization, rich search, ranking, recommendation, similarity
  and broad moderation remain above Core;
- SHA-256 BagIt is the minimum conventional exit adapter, not the Core model;
- personal/local-first is the mature media-use-case recommendation for `V2-F2`,
  while public-by-default remains the adopted classification policy; and
- no media-specific Core gap has survived the exact fixture pressure.

### Open engineering, not owner decisions yet

- independent implementation/OS reproduction of A-prime;
- canonical application Type/Record/Occurrence/Principal bytes and EFS
  projection after the generic profile gate exists;
- a Realm-qualified current Binding/fold and any bounded Lens point execution;
- live corrupt-primary/range fallback; encrypted export/import and leakage
  tests; booru/personal-manager round trips; 10k/1m indexes; and Stage B
  gas/state/interoperability measurements.
