## 1. Executive verdict, in plain language

**Arcade deserves the next month — as a labeled public demo and guest-UX probe, not as a validated community or EFS's permanent flagship.** The four statuses stay distinct: it is buildable as a *product demo* by Sept 11 on the v1 Sepolia stack; it is *partly* an engineering fixture; it has **not** earned *community pilot* (the corpus is EFS-assembled, and EFS's own July red team demoted exactly this shape); and it does **not** decide N5. The default is **GO-AS-DEMO-ONLY** with four evidence-gated GO conditions to upgrade ([[Designs/arcade/product-and-communities|product-and-communities]] §6). What makes it worth the month anyway: it is the only candidate that exercises the **instant-guest-deep-link** surface end to end with content strangers might actually click, it composes with (rather than competes against) the wiki and mod-capsule directions, and it produces the Devcon/grant demo artifact EFS needs regardless.

## 2. What is actually true about v1 today (all verified from source or live chain, 2026-08-07)

1. **`/games` is live on real Sepolia** — 15 games, IPFS-mirror-only, curator lens `0x11CbE1b6…9912`, seeded Jun 23–25 in 324 txs / 1,078 attestations. Measured cost ≈ 11.1M gas (~0.013 ETH) per game; the deployer holds 682 SepoliaETH.
2. **The contentHash convention is broken three ways, and the damage is durable**: canon is `f1220<sha256>` (specs/10, ratified 2026-06-20); the seeder wrote `0x`-keccak to **67 files on real Sepolia three days after ratification**; the SDK writes/verifies bare-sha256 and rejects both other forms; nothing anywhere reads or verifies hashes today. Mitigation: every file's canonical digest is recoverable trustlessly from its own on-chain CIDv1 mirror; re-binding is ~134 attestations. **The seeder fix is the single gate before any further durable seeding.**
3. **Stable game URLs exist; playable ones don't.** `/explorer/games/snake.html` survives the static export and eth.limo — and renders an empty grid (no file-leaf auto-open; confirmed in code and first-hand). Opening from the folder view executes HTML immediately in a `sandbox="allow-scripts"` srcDoc iframe — correctly isolated (ADR-0056), **never verified**, with no mirror fallback and no fetch timeout.
4. **The guest boot path is hostile**: 7 serial contract reads gate first paint; ~45 block-watchers per visitor; no multicall anywhere; the deployed build leans on public RPCs and observably failed during the hands-on probe. A public Arcade page must bypass the whole stack (pure-util reads + build-baked catalog + dedicated key — all Ephemeral-tier changes).
5. **Operational reality**: the June seed has **zero committed receipts**; the seeder tooling of record was never merged; the IPFS pins sit on **one** VPS Kubo node; the Sepolia faucet (needed for any visitor write) is built, integrated, and **not deployed**; devnet 26001993 currently has **no contracts at all**.

## 3. The product case, honestly

Demand: unproven and structurally hard — 12–18 lawful games versus itch.io's 711,932 free web games loses every fun/discovery contest; expect near-zero week-4 retention and measure curator/creator behavior instead. The only demand-side experiment EFS has run (the June–July buildathon) got low turnout and was wound down. What no incumbent provides (verified across a 15-platform matrix): **rehostability by strangers, verifiable bytes, curator plurality, no-single-owner durability** — all felt by the supply side (preservationists, curators, maintainers), not by players at click time — and of the four, **only curator plurality is a beat the conventional baseline cannot replicate**; mirror-kill/tamper/rebuild are parity-plus-identity and are labeled as such in the demo. Demand-shaped evidence exists at nerd altitude, read precisely: the Feb-2026 "HN Arcade" thread (352 points) mostly asked for discovery features, flagged dead links and bad metadata as trust-killers, and one established commenter asked for maintainer-independent stewardship. Crypto-aversion is majority-scale but specific (speculation, wallet-gating); js13kGames — the ideal supply pipeline — ran a NEAR/Protocol-Labs jam category, and is the beachhead (partnership ask retimed post-jam; the 2026 jam runs mid-Aug–mid-Sep). The honest pitch: *no wallet, no token, nothing for sale; the chain is the filing cabinet, not the product.*

## 6. Curation and comments recommendation

Curation: **trusted GitHub-PR data repo for September** (osgameclones shape, F-Droid-style label-don't-ban policy, Flashpoint-style tested-state tuples), publishing to EFS under the content lens with committed receipts, and a stated migration path to on-chain curator collections that preserves contributor history. Exercised end-to-end on one real candidate (Norman the Necromancer) before it's called a workflow. Comments: **the owner decides D2 explicitly** — the evidence says EFS-native-as-primary fails Sept 11 (one-click Sybil against a moderator who is away two weeks; no read indexer; permanent free-text exposure), so this pass recommends the reversible hybrid (giscus) plus a genuinely on-chain gesture (the star) plus an EFS archiver that makes the comment corpus durable and portable — centralized *for now, on purpose, with the exit designed*.

## 7. The permanence answer (draft for D7) and the sharpest risks

Draft public wording (corrected post-review to claim only what exists): *"Every game's identity, provenance, and curation history live on a public chain and in portable manifests; the bytes are mirrored and verifiable; if we disappear, anyone can rebuild this site from public records — that's the permanence we claim. Sepolia is a long-lived Ethereum testnet. The portable manifests carry no chain-specific values and are the rebuild source; migrating the on-chain history to a successor chain is planned but not yet designed — that work is tracked openly [link U17]. We promise the collection outlives us — not that any one server or chain lives forever."*

Sharpest remaining falsifiers: (1) **stable-URL honesty across the v2 recut** — v1 is officially "evidence, not baseline"; the portable-ID forwarding story is routed to the v2 designs as a named failing acceptance test; (2) **the manufactured-community risk** — if T1/T3 both fail, no amount of polish fixes it (PIVOT per plan); (3) **Sepolia-testnet objection** mishandled in public; (4) a **rights incident on permanent bytes** — structural prevention is license-clean intake, because EFS cannot offer Flashpoint's removal compromise; (5) the differentiator demo failing to land with normal viewers (U12 tests it before launch).
