## 1. Tag identity

| System | Identity | Normalisation | Rename |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Danbooru** | `tags(id, name UNIQUE, post_count, category int, is_deprecated, words[])` (structure.sql `CREATE TABLE public.tags`). But posts store `tag_string text` — space-separated **names**, and `tag_aliases`/`tag_implications` key on `antecedent_name`/`consequent_name` strings, not ids. The join key is the string. | `Tag.normalize_name` = `downcase.strip.tr(" ", "_")` (tag.rb:235-237). **Finding for the owner's "/clouds/nimbus" idea:** no booru encodes hierarchy as a path. Hierarchy is (a) flat namespaces (Hydrus `creator:`, `series:`…; e621 categories) and (b) an n→n implication DAG. Today's newest Danbooru implication is `kaiser_pmc_director_(hawaii)_(blue_archive) -> kaiser_pmc_director_(blue_archive)` (tag_implications.json, id 246196) — the "folder" is a parenthetical suffix plus an implication edge, and Hydrus states parents are n→n ("adjusting swimsuit implies both a swimsuit and adjusting clothes", advanced_parents.html, dated 2026-09-06). A single-parent `tagId = keccak(DOMAIN, parentTagId, keccak(name), kind)` (`Designs/efsv2/fable-handoff-v2-tag-core.md:70`) can name the tag but cannot carry booru "under" semantics; those must be separate directional edges, as `booru-app.md:128-137` already proposes.

## 2. Categories / types

- **Danbooru:** general 0, artist 1, copyright 3, character 4, meta 5 (tag_category.rb:9-13). Per-tag mutable column; who may change: admin any; Builder if `post_count < 1_000`; anyone if `< 50`; never on an artist tag with an artist entry (tag_policy.rb:4-9). Every name/category/deprecated change is versioned in `tag_versions(tag_id, version, name, category, is_deprecated, updater_id)` (structure.sql). A category change fans out: aliases pointing at the tag are recategorised (tag.rb:191-198) and every post's `tag_count_<category>` columns are recomputed by re-saving posts (tag.rb:167-185). Implications require equal categories (tag_implication.rb:110-115); a new umbrella tag inherits its child's category (149-153).
- **e621:** nine categories: general 0, artist 1, contributor 2, copyright 3, character 4, species 5, invalid 6, meta 7, lore 8 (tag_category.rb:4-26); invalid/meta/lore admin-only, lore names must end `_(lore)` (tag.rb:160-172, ADMIN_ONLY_MAPPING 94-104). "once a tag gets added to at least 100 posts, its category gets locked" → BUR (help/tags; `tag_type_change_cutoff = 100`, config:377-379); `is_locked` flag; changes logged to `tag_type_versions(old_type, new_type, is_locked)` (structure.sql). Alias approval will *not* recategorise a consequent with >10,000 posts (tag_alias.rb:453-459).
- **Hydrus:** category = namespace prefix, part of the string; PTR janitors "support" `creator: character: person: series: photoset: studio: species: title: medium: meta:`; others "liable to get siblinged out, removed, and/or mocked" (PTR.html).
- **szurubooru:** `tag_category(name, color, order, default)` table, FK from tag, freely editable (model/tag_category.py; func/tag_categories.py:81-89).
- **Gelbooru:** listing shows `artist | character | copyright | general | metadata` plus an `ambiguous` flag on some tags (`lollipop … general, ambiguous`).

## 3. Aliases and implications — data model, governance, and *when* they apply

**Danbooru (write-time materialisation + retroactive rewrite; aliases also expanded at read time).**
- Rows: `tag_aliases`/`tag_implications(antecedent_name, consequent_name, status ∈ active|deleted|retired, creator, approver, forum_topic, forum_post, reason)` (structure.sql; tag_relationship.rb:4). Both created through a Bulk Update Request whose script lines are `alias a -> b`, `imply a -> b`, `unalias`, `unimply`, `rename`, `update <search> -> <tags>`, `deprecate`, `category t -> c`, `nuke`, `convert pool` (help:bulk_update_requests; commands under `app/logical/bulk_update_request/command/`). A BUR opens a forum topic (bulk_update_request.rb:119-126), max 100 lines (processor.rb:7). Approval needs `user.level >= approval_level` (bur_policy.rb:14-18); default ADMIN, MODERATOR only when both sides are character tags with ≤200 posts (command/create_implication.rb:44-54; `SMALL_TAG_THRESHOLD = 200`, tag.rb:10). Approval flips status to `processing` and enqueues `ProcessBulkUpdateRequestJob` (bulk_update_request.rb:108-117; processor.rb:62-80).
- Validations: no cycles, no redundant transitive edge, neither side may itself be aliased (tag_implication.rb:67-106); alias chains `a->b, b->c` rejected unless the older one is auto-moved; reversing an alias auto-deletes the old one and any implication it replaces (tag_alias.rb:39-60). Size rules on request: antecedent ≥10 posts, ≥0.01 % of consequent, ≤90 % of `~a ~b`; both need wiki pages (tag_implication.rb:4-6, 121-145).
- **Write path:** on every post save `PostEdit#tag_names` computes `current + added − removed`, adds automatic tags, then `+= TagImplication.tags_implied_by(tag_names)` (post_edit.rb:44-50); user-added and user-removed names are first passed through `TagAlias.to_aliased` (83-99). `tags_implied_by` is a recursive CTE over active implications (tag_implication.rb:22-46). Result is written back as the sorted `tag_string` (post.rb:424-426). Implications are therefore *baked into every post's string*.
- **Retroactive:** `TagImplication#update_posts!` runs the search `"#{antecedent} -#{consequent}"` and re-saves each post under a row lock (tag_implication.rb:157-166) — one full tag_string rewrite per affected post. Alias approval = `TagMover.move!` (tag_alias.rb:35-37), i.e. every post carrying the old name is retagged (tag_mover.rb:73-82); blacklists and saved searches are string-rewritten (134-142).
- **Removal:** `reject!` only sets `status = "deleted"` and logs a mod action (tag_relationship.rb:58-65). No code path re-touches posts. Consequent tags already materialised stay on posts indistinguishable from user edits; the implication merely stops being added to future saves. A weekly job additionally *retires* aliases to empty tags, implications from empty tags, and general/artist aliases unused for 2 years (tag_relationship_retirement_service.rb, `THRESHOLD = 2.years`).
- **Read path:** searches resolve aliases again: `PostQuery.normalize → replace_aliases` via `TagAlias.aliases_for` (post_query.rb:32-46, 196-200, 219-221); `unaliased:` metatag bypasses it (post_query_builder.rb:37). Implications are **not** expanded at query time — `tags_include` is a bare array-containment on `tag_string` (post.rb:1762-1764).

**e621 (same model, hardened; adds cached closure, undo, Sidekiq).**
- `tag_implications.descendant_names text[]` caches the transitive closure per row and is recomputed for all parents on save (tag_implication.rb:6, 21-85; a circular chain sets `status: "error: circular implication detected"`, 74-84). Help: "This implication process occurs AFTER the alias process… only an administrator can approve them" (help/tag_implications, today); aliases: "whenever someone searched for girl or tagged a post with girl, it would be internally replaced with female" (help/tag_aliases).
- **Write path** (`Post#normalize_tags`, post.rb:708-736): scan → metatags → downcase → strip negated (`-tag`, alias-resolved) → strip DNP tags → `TagAlias.to_aliased` → locked tags → automatic tags → `TagImplication.with_descendants` → DNP-lock → `find_or_create_by_name_list` → drop invalid → sorted `tag_string`. Locked-tag removals also remove the tag's cached descendants (716). Max 2,000 tags per post (config:503-505; post.rb:697-705).
- **Retroactive:** `approve!` sets `queued` and enqueues `TagImplicationJob` (Sidekiq queue `tags`, `lock: :until_executed`, 24 h lock; tag_implication_job.rb). `process!` snapshots undo data in chunks of `POST_LIMIT` posts *before* mutating, then `Post.sql_raw_tag_match(antecedent).find_each` re-saves each post lacking the consequent with `do_not_version_changes = true` and `skip_post_index_update`, retries 5× with `sleep 2**tries`, then `TagImplicationFinalizeJob` re-indexes (tag_implication.rb:127-209). Alias `process!` additionally moves relationships, fixes category, rewrites `locked_tags` and every user's `blacklisted_tags` string (tag_alias.rb:383-414, 424-483; `to_aliased_query` 144-204), renames the artist entry.
- **Removal:** e621 *can* undo: `undo!` replays the snapshot, removing only tags "still there" that another active implication doesn't re-add during the same save (tag_implication.rb:217-219, 265-332; alias 206-286). The comment at tag_alias.rb:206-215 concedes the limit: "Once the alias has rewritten them, an occurrence of the consequent tag can no longer be told apart from one the user put there on purpose" — the inherent cost of write-time materialisation.
- **Read path:** query terms are alias-resolved (`TagQuery.normalize`, tag_query.rb:481-488; must/must_not/should each through `TagAlias.to_aliased`, 1710-1712); no implication expansion. The cheatsheet documents the consequence: `breasts -huge_breasts … also removes posts with hyper_breasts` because chains are materialised (help/cheatsheet, "Implication Chains").

**Hydrus (virtual at storage, materialised in a derived display cache).**
- Siblings: `A->B` n→1, transitive, acyclic ("a tag cannot have more than one ideal… non-cyclic--no loops", advanced_siblings). Parents: `C->P` n→n, acyclic, "virtual. P is not actually added to every file by C, it just appears as if it is… If you remove a C->P parent relationship, all the implied P tags will disappear!" (advanced_parents). Yet adding parents is retroactive and heavy: "the client will look up all the files with all your added tag Cs and retroactively apply/pend the respective tag Ps if needed. This could mean thousands of tags!… It also takes a bunch of CPU."
- Mechanically the client keeps per-(file service, tag service) tables `display_current_mappings(hash_id, tag_id)` / `display_pending_mappings` (ClientDBMappingsCacheSpecificDisplay.py:83-84), populated by `AddImplications` ("for all new implications, get files with those tags and not existing", 225-283) and kept in sync by a background `TagDisplayMaintenanceManager` with work/rest throttling (ClientTagsHandling.py:355-650). "Ideal" vs "actual" sibling/parent lookup caches record the not-yet-applied delta (ClientDBTagSiblings.py:157-158, 409-426; ClientDBTagParents.py:144, 397-414). So Hydrus is: storage never rewritten; a *derived, rebuildable* materialisation serves reads; removal = drop derived rows, storage untouched. The API says the same: "'storage_tags' represent the actual tags stored… 'display_tags' reflect how tags appear in the UI, after siblings are collapsed and parents are added" (developer_api.html:2381) and "File searches occur in the display tag_display_type" (:2016).
- PTR governance: "Adding tags pass automatically. Deleting (petitioning) tags requires janitor action… Adding and petitioning siblings and parents all require janitor action. The client always assumes the server approves any petition. If your petition gets rejected you wont know"; >2 weeks out of sync → cannot push (PTR.html).

**szurubooru (write-time, client-side, not retroactive).** `tag_implication(parent_id, child_id)` and `tag_suggestion` are id-keyed many-to-many (model/tag.py:7-55). The server's `update_post_tags` only resolves names to tags — no implication step (func/posts.py:698-704). Implications are added by the *web client* when a tag is typed: `TagList.addByName(tagName, addImplications)` fetches the tag and recursively adds `implications` (client/js/models/tag_list.js:40-58). Creating an implication later does nothing to existing posts; the API has no job. Aliases need no expansion anywhere: search and edit both resolve any `tag_name` row to the tag id.

**Gelbooru:** aliases exist, "must be approved by an administrator" (alias list page); no implication feature on any first-party page found; `~` OR "Currently does not work" (help cheatsheet).

## 4. Search and indexing

- **Danbooru grammar:** space = AND, `or`/`~` = OR, `-` = NOT, parentheses, `*` wildcards (expanded to the 100 largest matching tags, `MAX_WILDCARD_TAGS`, post_query_builder.rb:14, 117-120), ranges `1..2`, `>=`, `a,b,c`; metatag list at post_query_builder.rb:31-38, orders 40-67. AST → CNF (post_query.rb:214-216) → SQL: AND = `string_to_array(posts.tag_string,' ') @> ARRAY[...]` (`where_array_includes_all`), wildcard = `&&` any, NOT = `negate_relation` on the containment predicate, OR = relation `.or` (post_query_builder.rb:106-135). Index: `CREATE INDEX index_posts_on_string_to_array_tag_string ON public.posts USING gin (string_to_array(tag_string, ' '))` (structure.sql:5532). No tsvector; no Elasticsearch. NOT is evaluated by Postgres as a filter over the positive scan; there is an explicit planner hack forcing bitmap scans for searches under `small_search_threshold` or containing deprecated tags (post_query_builder.rb:176-223). **Limits:** 2 tags Member, 6 Gold, unlimited Platinum (user.rb:682-692); free metatags (post_query.rb:10-14); statement timeouts 3 s / 6 s / 9 s (user.rb:662-672); ordering/limit/random are singleton, non-negatable, non-OR-able (324-337). Counts: `post_count` incremented/decremented per save (tag.rb:84-91); multi-tag counts use EXPLAIN row estimates, cached 5 min (post_query.rb:246-315). Autocomplete: trigram GIN on `name`, tsvector GIN over `words`, GIN over word initials (structure.sql:5862-5911).
- **e621:** posts also keep `tag_string` + the same GIN (structure.sql:5524) but *search runs in OpenSearch*: mapping `tags: keyword`, `tag_count_<cat>` ints, 5 shards/1 replica, `max_result_window 250_000` (post_index.rb:1-80); tags become `term: {tags: x}` under `must` / `must_not` / `should` (elastic_post_query_builder.rb:45-48). Grammar adds parenthesised groups with mandatory spaces, nesting to `DEPTH_LIMIT` (tag_query.rb:31, 538-611), `~` OR, `-` NOT, one positive wildcard per search, unlimited negated wildcards, **40 tags per search** (`tag_query_limit`, config:382-384; cheatsheet). Postgres counting is explicitly "extraordinarily expensive… only… a background job" (tag.rb:78-84); counts come from OpenSearch (74-76).
- **Hydrus:** AND default; OR by shift-entering an "OR chain"; `-` negation; `*` wildcards ("Every normal autocomplete search has a secret * on the end"); namespace searches; `system:` predicates; search domain = file service × tag service, default "all known tags" = union across services; "Tags will be searchable by all their siblings" (getting_started_searching, lines 250-283). Storage is SQLite mapping tables plus the display caches above.
- **szurubooru:** tokens split on whitespace; `-` negates; `key:value` named tokens; **comma inside a value = OR** (`ArrayCriterion`), `..` = range, `-min/-max` suffixes, `sort:`, `special:` (search/parser.py). An anonymous token is a tag-name subquery over `post_tag ⋈ tag ⋈ tag_name` with `*` → SQL `LIKE %` (post_search_config.py:218-225; util.py:42-49); `category:` filter (380). Plain Postgres/SQLAlchemy, no external index.
- **Gelbooru:** `tag1 tag2`, `-tag`, `ta*1`, fuzzy `night~`, `user: md5: rating: parent: width: height: score: sort:field:asc|desc`; "combining the same metatags… will not work"; score "updated once daily at 12AM CST" (help cheatsheet; howto:search wiki updated 2023-11-08).

## 5. Wiki, history, counts, related tags, deprecated, DNP

- **Wiki:** Danbooru/e621 `wiki_pages` keyed by `title = tag name` (tag.rb `has_one :wiki_page, foreign_key: "title"`); merged on alias (tag_mover.rb:177-191: old body becomes "This tag has been moved to [[…]]"). Implications require wikis on both sides (Danbooru). szurubooru: `tag.description` only.
- **History:** Danbooru `tag_versions`; e621 `tag_type_versions` + `avoid_posting_versions`; post tag edits in `post_versions` (e621's daily export of that table alone is 9.57 GB gzip). Hydrus: PTR is an append-only update stream ("downloading and processing the entire tag database and history of the PTR", PTR.html). szurubooru: optimistic `version` int, no history table.
- **Related tags:** Danbooru samples 5,000 posts of the search (ordered by md5), counts co-occurring tags, ranks by cosine similarity, caches 8 h (related_tag_calculator.rb:11-17, 65, 128-145); sidebar categories per tag type (tag_category.rb `search_sidebar_tag_categories`). e621 precomputes `related_tags` as a string *on the tag row* via a job with expiry `sqrt(post_count)` hours clamped 24–720 (tag.rb:281-317; visible in `tags.json`: `"anthro 300 mammal 247 hi_res 221 …"`). szurubooru computes siblings live by co-occurrence, top 50 (func/tags.py:207-223).
- **Deprecated / invalid / ambiguous:** Danbooru `is_deprecated` — deprecated tags cannot be *added* (post_edit.rb:76-79, 119-121; help:tags), only admins may toggle, and only if a wiki listing replacements exists (tag_policy.rb:11-15); BUR `deprecate`. e621 uses an `invalid` category (help/tags). Gelbooru has an `ambiguous` flag. Disambiguation everywhere is a naming convention (`orange_(fruit)`, `_(cosplay)` special-cased in the validator, lines 48-58).
- **DNP:** Danbooru: artist ban = mass-update `status:banned` over the artist's posts + `is_banned` on the artist row (artist.rb:227-233). e621: `avoid_postings(artist_id, details, staff_notes, is_active)` with versions (structure.sql; avoid_posting.rb); the `avoid_posting`/`conditional_dnp` tags are typed *artist* so they surface, are stripped if user-added and re-added only via implication from the artist tag, then locked (post.rb:741-760; tag_category.rb:109). The wiki page `avoid_posting` lists the implicating artist tags — by eye on the order of a thousand entries today (not counted precisely).

## 7. Hydrus multi-authority resolution (the Lens analogue)

- Each tag service has an **ordered application list**: `tag_sibling_application(master_service_id, service_index, application_service_id)` and the same for parents (ClientDBTagSiblings.py:112; ClientDBTagParents.py:98); default is the service applying only itself (ClientDBTagSiblings.py:350). Docs: "The service at the top of the list has precedence over all else, then second, and so on… If you for example have PTR you can use a tag service to overwrite tags/siblings for cases where you disagree with the PTR standards" (getting_started_more_tags.html, line 262); the parents doc shows exactly this pattern (a local "my parent favourites" service ranked above the PTR).
- **Conflict rule for siblings:** `Regen` iterates applicable services in index order, skips pairs the user has petitioned, includes pending pairs, and feeds each into `TagSiblingsStructure.AddPair` (ClientDBTagSiblings.py:986-1020). `AddPair` refuses `A->A`, refuses "A -> C when A -> B already exists", and drops cycles (ClientTagsHandling.py:1020-1050). Net effect: **first service in the list wins per bad tag** — precisely first-attester-wins keyed on the *bad* tag. Chains extend transitively and re-point ideals when a chain's end gains a new ideal (1052-1075).
- **Parents:** union of ancestors/descendants across services with loops dropped (TagParentsStructure.AddPair, ClientTagsHandling.py:935-990) — no precedence needed because parents are n→n. Parent definitions are computed *after* sibling collapse ("all parent definitions are sibling collapsed, so are terminus of their sibling chains", ClientDBTagDisplay.py:246); a sibling change therefore triggers parent regeneration (669-686).
- **Cross-service merge for display/search:** "there is no concept of 'all known tags' here. If a tag is in 'my tags', it follows the rules of 'my tags', and then all the services' display tags are merged into the 'all known tags' pool" (developer_api.html:1538). The author warns clients off recomputing this ("I generally warn you off computing sibling and parent mappings or counts yourself", :1549) — the union is the client's job and is itself materialised in the combined-files display cache.

This is the closest existing implementation of the vault's Lens (`fable-handoff-v2-tag-core.md:33` "ordered trusted-attester list; first-attester-wins"): the ordered list resolves n→1 relations (siblings ≈ REDIRECT), unions n→n relations (parents ≈ implication edges), and materialises the result per (file domain, tag domain) pair.

## 8. Table stakes, and what each costs on-chain

**Bottom line.** Danbooru and e621 — the two systems everyone benchmarks against — are pure write-time materialisers keyed on *name strings*, and their hardest engineering (TagMover, Sidekiq jobs, undo snapshots, 9.6 GB of post_versions) exists to pay for that choice. Hydrus is the only one that keeps storage immutable and derives display, and it is also the only one with a working pluralistic-authority rule; its cost is a CPU-heavy background sync and a client that is told not to recompute the merge itself. For EFS the evidence points to: immutable tag records + REDIRECT aliases resolved at read; implication edges as separate n→n records resolved at read for humans; a *separately-kept* derived bitmap, maintained by word-wise OR rather than per-post rewrites, only for the few hot ancestors contracts must probe; counts and related tags derived, never stored per post.
