# J2 — Cypherpunk / long-horizon risk judgement of the EFS 2.0 direction (2026-09-02)

**Lens:** the 50-year promise — permanence, exit, censorship resistance, independent reconstruction, honest `UNKNOWN`, no hidden hosted authority, and the "confirmed, then unreadable" failure shape.
**Inputs:** all 22 Phase 1 reader maps (R1–R20 incl. R7a/b, R11a/b), the eleven core documents named in the task, and targeted re-reads of the vault where a claim is load-bearing (cited inline). Vault read-only; nothing under the planning vault was touched.
**Verdict in one line:** the *shape* is right and unusually honest; three of its promises cannot be kept as written on the venue class it names, and the vault has not told the owner so.

Net: as a *shape* the direction is the best thing in the vault. The problems are not with the shape; they are with three promises the shape cannot keep on the venue class it names, and with the process that has stopped recording what the owner has said.

### 1.2 Wrong, or unkeepable as written

**(a) The permanence promise is per-Realm and operator-conditional, and nobody has told the owner or the user.** The unit of deployment is "a fresh qualifying L3" (`system-constitution.md` line 303 "Fresh qualifying L3"; `owner-rulings.md` 2026-08-12 lines 184–187 "'Fresh L3' means a qualifying EVM Realm can stand alone"). The 2026-07-10 ruling "assume a blockchain persists indefinitely and stays queryable … DROP … chain-death machinery" (`owner-rulings.md` lines 11–12) was adopted in a home-chain world and is never restated or retired in the spine (grep for persist/dies/sunset/dead-chain over `system-constitution.md` and `core-architecture-candidate.md`: no hits, verified 2026-09-02). "Qualifying" is undefined anywhere current (constitution lines 38, 60, 303, 353). The evidence round then found the venue class the design names is the mortal one: "The 'chains don't die' ruling (2026-07-10) is safe for L1, arguable for the top L2s, and empirically false for L3s" (`Reviews/2026-08-13-claude-evidence-round/corpus/venue/commons-realm-venue-matrix.md` §1 item 7); "Every venue except L1 has exit window 'None' … the L2 keys can change the machine underneath your frozen contracts" (§1 item 3; `CORRECTIONS.md` row "Every L2 has no exit window": no *surveyed* L2 met a 30-day bar under all upgrade paths); AnyTrust L3s "cannot reconstruct chain state from parent-chain data alone" (§1 item 6). Stage A's honest answer is that persistence is "a named qualifying assumption per Realm … the Realm's operator asserts by deploying" (`b0-realm-admission.md` lines 365–380) and its proposed constitution text says outright: "A throwaway devnet or short-lived fork can run Core, but no EFS promise survives its operator abandoning it. Deployment diligence, not protocol machinery, is the answer to Realm mortality" (`Reviews/2026-08-13-efs2-stage-a-corpus/corpus/proposed-spine-edits.md` A2, lines 120–137). That text is **unadopted** (`STATUS.md` line 46 "No Stage A proposal is adopted into `Designs/efsv2/`"), and the owner has never been asked the three questions the venue memo isolates: does an Etched kernel tolerate a mutable machine underneath it; does "reconstructible" mean from venue state or from the parent after the venue dies; is "chains don't die" retained, scoped, or retired (`commons-realm-venue-matrix.md` §6 items 1–3). **This is the deepest hole from my lens: the 50-year promise, the no-elision "ETCH IT" promise, and "independent reconstruction" are all conditional on a live RPC for a Realm whose operator may leave — and the reconstruction walk itself says so: "this walk reconstructs state at the basis the RPC serves … Reconstructing a past snapshot needs archive state — a bonus … never a requirement" (`b0-realm-admission.md` §8.2 lines 1821–1824).** There is no dead-Realm export artifact anywhere current (`.efs-bundle`/Century Bundle exist only in July evidence — R20 F12, R5 F10, R11b §3 B10). Direction: partly wrong until the owner scopes the promise (L1-or-exported vs per-Realm-conditional) and the constitution says it.

**(b) "Independent reconstruction" cannot re-verify who signed.** "Core intentionally does **not** persist the main envelope witness. `getEnvelopeBytes` therefore cannot replay a historic signature … Historical authorship grade comes from the receipt's immutable admission basis" and "absent an externally supplied original witness, historic signature verification is not replayable from state and is not claimed" (`Reviews/2026-08-13-efs2-stage-a-corpus/chapters/b0-authorship-envelope.md` lines 766–774, 1471–1474). So a second implementation reconstructs *that this Core said an author signed* — a trust-me fact about the authority layer of a system whose founding rule is verify-don't-trust. The owner paid for bodies ("full-body spine: RULED — PAY IT", `owner-rulings.md` line 67) and was never offered the witness (65 bytes, three slots per EOA envelope) while the same design retains up to 7,808 bytes of pre-withdrawal evidence per effective T4 (`b0-authorship-envelope.md` lines 1474–1476; R7a F8). For ERC-1271 accounts replay is genuinely impossible (code changes), so the receipt basis is right *there*; for EOAs it is a choice nobody made deliberately. Direction: wrong by omission; cheap to fix; must be an owner ask because it is "PAY IT"-class.

**(c) Storage-heavy Etched promises are priced against a schedule that is leaving.** EIP-8037 appears nowhere in `Designs/` or the Stage A corpus (`grep -rn 8037` on both: no hits, verified 2026-09-02). The mandatory bundle is SSTORE-new dominated by construction: "reversible log: 2 × SSTORE-new ≈ 44,200; fresh OccStatus: 1 × SSTORE-new ≈ 22,100; posting append: amortized ≈ 13,420 each … typical leaf ≈ 187,100 … worst leaf ≈ 656,800" (`Reviews/2026-08-13-efs2-stage-a-corpus/chapters/b0-indexes.md` §9 lines 1826–1859, all `[HYPOTHESIS]`). The evidence round's qualified figure is "about 4.9× for a net-new storage-slot component. Full EFS transaction impact … must be benchmarked; L2 adoption was unverified" (`CORRECTIONS.md` row "EIP-8037"). R7a's arithmetic (F2) shows a maximal 8,192-byte body would exceed the EIP-7825 cap at that repricing. The ONE gas snapshot that the 2026-07-15 ruling named as "Blocker to final sign" (`owner-rulings.md` line 69) has never been produced (`STATUS.md` lines 50–52 "No gas/state-growth measurement … exists"). Harmless on near-free Sepolia; fatal to falsifier 14 ("aggregate gas/state … not economically credible on the intended L2/L3 profile", `core-architecture-candidate.md` lines 437–438) if measured only on today's schedule. Direction: not wrong, but the owner's "bigger on-chain is the bet" (`owner-rulings.md` line 36) got ~5× worse and the ledger does not say so.

**(d) The mandatory-indexing metadata consequence is ruled; its named mitigation is designed nowhere.** "on-chain = metadata-exposed, full stop; the client is the only true metadata-privacy path" (`owner-rulings.md` line 62) is honest and correct. The "sensitivity policy layer" the same owner named as the mitigation (line 32, "a named subsystem, not a footnote") has zero hits in any `Designs/web-client-os/*.md` (R4 F7, R8 F13). For a public File Browser MVP this is not blocking — "public permanence" is in the plan preview (`mvp-and-acceptance.md` line 72) — but the Files draft's fail-closed default on `UNCLASSIFIED_SENSITIVE` would block a guest-promoted writer with no profile (`hierarchical-files-and-folders.md` §11, per R3 §7). The honest MVP line is "everything you write here is public, permanent, and force-indexed", said once, in the ceremony.

**(e) The policy rulings that make EFS cypherpunk live outside the ledger, and their standing after the reset is unstated.** "EFS is a permanent archive — everyone pays for the data they write to the chain; there is no free ephemeral tier" (`Designs/efsv2/efs-substrate-decision.md` line 61, RULED 2026-07-02); "Fully permissionless byte pool — RULED (no gating) … there is no protocol takedown … Neutrality is preserved at the *protocol* layer; filtering lives entirely at the *edges*" (`Designs/efsv2/large-file-uploads.md` line 78, 2026-07-07); the durability tiering "on-chain > Arweave > grant-pinned IPFS > volunteer IPFS" (`owner-rulings.md` line 37). The ledger begins 2026-07-10; the 2026-08-12 reset "supersedes earlier mechanism-level rulings, not the problems they were solving" (`owner-rulings.md` lines 178–179) without saying which of these *policy* rulings survive; the constitution's nearest sentence is "Permanent-public-data tools warn and may refuse obvious hazard classes at the client edge; Core remains neutral evidence infrastructure" (lines 263–264) — compatible, but not a restatement. These are the sentences a hostile reviewer will ask about first; they should be ledgered or retired, not inferred.

**(f) The first write-capable product re-introduces "stolen EOA = permanent capture" unstated.** The MVP defers rotation/recovery (constitution lines 137–139 "A full custom KEL is not frozen into the MVP"; `owner-decision-inbox.md` P-8). Its directory entries are Principal-owned CAS Binding heads ("Admission derives `principalId` from the authored Occurrence, so a writer cannot bind another Principal's key", `core-architecture-candidate.md` lines 222–223). B0: "an ungraduated `AccountPrincipal` has **no rotation** — its authority reference is immutable, so key theft means permanent capture of that PrincipalId's future writes" (`b0-principal-authority.md` §6.3 per R7a; R20 F5). The seam is correctly reserved (graduation G1–G8; `(authorityRef, authEpoch)` in the envelope; `authorityKind` append-only). The *statement* to the first user, and an acceptance-table row for key loss, are missing (constitution acceptance table lines 301–318 has no key-loss trace). Direction: right to defer; wrong to defer silently.

**(g) Sandboxed third-party execution is correctly a lane, not a promise — but one product set still sells the promise.** `app-runtime-and-direct-launch.md` labels an iframe with open egress `DIRECT_EGRESS` / `MUTABLE_REMOTE_EXECUTION_OR_DATA` and says it "loses exact-execution, offline and reproducibility qualifications" (lines 644–661, per R9/R14). The Arcade set still sells "verifies ✓" and "at least one visibly EFS-only behavior (a mirror dies live; the link keeps working)" (`Designs/arcade/README.md` line 31) — a claim its own product doc calls "parity beats plus on-chain identity" and the falsification round classified as baseline (`CORRECTIONS.md` row "'0 EFS-specific'…"; R18 F4, R14 F4/F10). The Safari measurement — "a three-second busy loop in the opaque child froze Safari's host page"; "~22 animation frames per second" (`Reviews/2026-08-13-claude-evidence-round/README.md` lines 138–141) — is bound into no current runtime doc (R9 F3, R18 F5). Direction: keep apps out of the MVP; never let a sandbox label become a permanence or exactness claim.

**(h) Hidden hosted authority: clean, with one seam to watch.** Nothing in the current spine makes a hosted service correct-by-definition: the guest path forbids hosted indexers (constitution lines 66–70), The Graph is last-resort by a dated media ruling (`Designs/media-library/owner-rulings.md` 2026-08-14), public IPFS gateways are named non-critical (`Reviews/2026-08-24-ipfs-maintainership-transition.md` "A direct guest path cannot depend on a public utility"). The one seam: complete directory listing has no Core primitive ("neither is current B0", `Designs/efsv2/README.md` lines 86–89; "Current B0 can read a Binding head and history only after the caller knows the BindingKey … A hosted index or opaque live manifest would violate independent reconstruction", `hierarchical-files-and-folders.md` §5.1 lines 660–667). The MVP must therefore ship a *labelled `PARTIAL`* listing before it ships an index — never a hosted index. `mvp-and-acceptance.md` already forbids the wrong answer (line 827 "UI calls a partial set complete or relies on a private index" is the redesign trigger); the risk is the temptation, not the text.

**(i) The process itself is a "confirmed, then unreadable" pattern.** Twenty-eight owner directions dated 2026-08-14→23 exist only in a `#status/draft` README (`Designs/web-client-os/README.md` lines 35–170); the append-only ledger ends 2026-08-12; the constitution still asks whether the first Web Client ships writes (line 358) after the owner answered (direction 2, lines 44–47); Stage A's 16 spine edits are unapplied; all three In Flight cards are expired (`Kanban.md` lines 37, 40, 43); `Open-Decisions.md` says "Ask now: 0" (line 8) while the only decision that starts an MVP — authorize the disposable Stage B and the File Browser fixture, name the repo and the Realm — has no bucket (R19 F8). A 50-year project whose canonical record stops recording is a permanence risk in miniature.

**Verdict:** `mostly-right-but-overscoped` — right shape, three unkeepable-as-written promises (a, b, c), one silent risk (f), and a product/process surface (g, i) that has grown far past the thing it is supposed to ship.

## 2. Top risks to an MVP starting now (ranked)

1. **No Core exists and no one owns making one.** "No Solidity, TypeScript, or Rust prototype has executed the corpus" (`STATUS.md` line 51); the Kanban Core card expired 2026-08-16 with "no owner ask" (`Kanban.md` line 43); the client set waits "after explicit experiment authorization" (`Designs/web-client-os/README.md` lines 466–472) and forbids every repository name (lines 496–498); two incompatible Stage B programs exist (the 9-cell bakeoff vs the 2026-08-14 superpowers plan, per R6 §7). Every product adapter adapts to nothing.
2. **The permanence/exit promise is unscoped on the venue class the MVP will use.** Sepolia is a testnet with documented read-path mortality (`CORRECTIONS.md` rows on Goerli/Holesky; validator permissioning "unresolved"). A debugging MVP will burn Realms: "Any change outside U-1(a-e) is semantics-breaking and MUST NOT reuse the RealmId … Core has no successor pointer and no admin successor bit" (`b0-realm-admission.md` U-3 lines 1416–1427); RealmId distribution is "above Core" (lines 350–354). The first user-visible links will point at dead Realms with no ceremony for saying so (R7a F14).
3. **Complete listing / read-after-create has no Core primitive and no owner** (§1.2 h). Adding an index kind after genesis is exactly the "PARTIAL reads as absence" hazard the design forbids ("`BindingScope` must exist at Realm genesis", `hierarchical-files-and-folders.md` lines 699–701).
4. **Byte carrier for guest read-back is undecided while the inherited default is being defunded.** "Which exact byte carrier and retention receipt can support clean-browser file read-back without becoming correctness authority?" is open (`mvp-and-acceptance.md` lines 898–899); "Admission succeeding while all carriers fail leaves a real FileRevision with `BYTES_UNAVAILABLE` … not a passing file-create acceptance result" (lines 271–274); Shipyard stops operating `ipfs.io`/`dweb.link`/bootstrap nodes on 2026-09-30 (`Reviews/2026-08-24-ipfs-maintainership-transition.md` §"Verified event"); the carrier-extinction trace was routed to a card that had already expired (R18 F12). State-tier custody is "an optional venue module" in B0 and the default 256 KiB chunk "cannot reach state-tier custody" (`b0-content-locators.md` lines 757, 767, per R3).
5. **Two wallet signatures per Files write, unacknowledged.** "`admitAsSender` implicit intent … is legal **only when** the selected set contains none of the three kernel-effect Types … BindingSet and BindingTombstone require explicit intent plus `expectedRevisions`" (`b0-overview.md` SR-12 lines 336–344); the MVP says "sign the authored `PublicationEnvelope` and the Realm-bound `AdmissionIntent` separately" (`mvp-and-acceptance.md` lines 74–78). No doc states the cost, the half-signed cancel case, or what the person keeps (R11b F3/F4, R3 F16).
6. **Unmintable Codex.** `ERC1271_VERIFY_GAS = MEASUREMENT_PENDING` makes the Authority Codex unmintable and therefore every `RealmId` uncomputable (R7a F5). Trivial to pick; unowned.
7. **EIP-8037 unmodelled** (§1.2 c): falsifier 14 can pass today and fail after Glamsterdam.
8. **Type arm unchosen; Files is B0-only; the client adapter is C-shaped; two names and two `RecordId` preimages** (R1 F4, R2 F1/F2, R10 F1). Disposable is fine; "disposable" must be visible in the bytes (domain strings) so nobody links to them.
9. **Stolen-EOA capture** (§1.2 f) for the first real writer.
10. **The ledger gap** (§1.2 i): directions 2/7/8/9/10 bind Core and are unrecorded; V2-E1/E2/E6 and the constitution contradict them.

## 4. Solid enough to build on now

- The one-sentence model and the five-way separation `RecordId ≠ OccurrenceRef ≠ Admission ≠ Binding head ≠ Lens result` (`core-architecture-candidate.md` lines 38–44); falsifiers 1–14 (lines 420–438).
- Full-width `bytes32 PrincipalId` end to end; `RecordId` independent of the Envelope; state-readable bodies and no elision; mandatory indexing per admitted Type/index profile.
- The honest-read vocabulary: `COMPLETE/PARTIAL/UNSUPPORTED/UNKNOWN` with Realm, basis, high-water mark, cursor, coverage; "`UNKNOWN` is never absence" (constitution lines 205–209) — carried consistently into Files, App Store, and the client's `ResourceOutcome<T>`/`ByteOutcome`/`PlanOutcome` law (R10 §3, R8 §12).
- Guest read with zero wallet/Commons/OS/indexer, EIP-1898-pinned reads, RPC miss ≠ absence (`mvp-and-acceptance.md` §A boxes 1–4, 8–9).
- Locators plural and never identity; verify before use; blobs never custody; the IPFS carrier-extinction trace as an acceptance test (`Reviews/2026-08-24-ipfs-maintainership-transition.md` §"Remaining operational gap").
- Stage A discipline as a *disposable* baseline: SR-1 domain table, chain-free EIP-712 envelope digest and `EnvelopeId`, `AdmissionIntent/1` digest and 2-D nonce lanes, the occurrence state machine T1–T6, Binding CAS T1–T9 with no-resurrection, `RealmId/profileId/genesisCommitment/RealmRevisionId`, the reconstruction read ABI W-0..W-10, QR-1..QR-8 as *named* assumptions, `UNAVAILABLE_SOURCE_BASIS` (R6 §11, R7a §13, R7b §10).
- Risk-bearer rule; discovery never authorizes; no ambient authority; conserved System Chrome vs replaceable Shell; storage-loss-as-named-event (R11b §10).
- The 2026-08-08 and 2026-08-12 rulings themselves (attributed, dated, unambiguous).

## 6. My MVP proposal (propose, not adopt)

1. **Scope:** one *disposable* Realm on Sepolia (every domain string tagged `efs2-exp/…`, never `efs2/…`; README sentence "this Realm will be discarded"), one atomic B0-derived Core: `publish`; EOA-only `AccountPrincipal/1`; Records, Envelope, Occurrence; admission receipts with revision basis; Binding CAS (`BindingSet/1`, `BindingTombstone/1`, `Withdrawal/1`); baseline indexes (by Type/Record/Principal + admission log) **plus one Binding-scope index at genesis**; a 1-entry `ResolutionPlan/1` resolver; a state-tier byte store for files ≤ ~20 KiB. **Persist the EOA witness** (three slots) so a second implementation re-verifies authorship from state.
2. **Client:** static, self-hostable guest reader — exact route with chain/Core/Realm/basis as separate fields, EIP-1898-pinned reads, zero wallet code on the guest path, listing labelled `COMPLETE` or `PARTIAL` (never empty), exact `FileRevision`, bytes from Realm state verified against `ChunkTree`; then explicit write promotion: New folder / New file / Publish revision with an EIP-6963 EOA, two EIP-712 signatures stated as the MVP cost, sequential submission, canonical read-back, `EXPERIMENTAL_DIRECT_CORE` labels digest-bound into the receipt.
3. **Order:** (i) mint disposable Codex constants, Files Types, and a genesis manifest (`BindingScope` included); (ii) Solidity Core + TypeScript reader; (iii) clean-room second reader (Rust or independent TS) that rebuilds Types/Records/Occurrences/Bindings/listing **and re-verifies every EOA witness** from state alone; (iv) guest-read fixture incl. the IPFS-extinction trace (trivially satisfied because bytes are in state); (v) write fixture; (vi) gas snapshot under current **and** EIP-8037 schedules; (vii) Realm-churn drill: deploy Realm 2, prove every Realm-1 link reports `UNSUPPORTED`/`UNKNOWN`, never empty.
4. **Behind an adapter:** Type arm (B0 control); Principal surface (uniform `PrincipalId` via SDK normalization); byte carrier (state tier now; `Locator/1`/`DurabilityGrade/1` minted but unused); Lens size; wallet stack.
5. **Frozen:** nothing. "Frozen-for-the-experiment" only, and visibly so in the bytes.
6. **Explicitly not:** Arcade/App Store/Media/Git lanes; managed Principals; mounts; encrypted profiles; Commons venue; any 64-Principal measurement in the client; Service Worker; OS generations; any permanence claim about Sepolia; any hosted index, ever.
7. **What this MVP proves for the 50-year promise:** that a stranger with the Realm's state and the static client can rebuild the graph, re-verify who signed, list a directory honestly, fetch bytes that share the Realm's fate, and see an honest failure when the Realm is gone — the only four properties that matter at year fifty. Everything else is a product lane.
