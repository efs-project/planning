## Verdict: **strained**

The evidence round is cited correctly everywhere it is cited (as dated evidence, through `CORRECTIONS.md`), and the *shape* of three of its five load-bearing findings is already in the current design: blobs are not custody, a sandbox is not a network cage, `UNKNOWN` is never absence. What the design has not absorbed is any of the *numbers* or the *questions the numbers raise*. `8037`, `22 fps`, `exit window`, and `97,920` have zero hits under `Designs/`. The three gates the round was routed to (V2-E4, V2-E7, and the held Arcade recut) carry text reconciled on 2026-08-12 — the day before the round landed — and none was amended. The result is that the storage-heaviest design choices in the vault (full-body spine, no elision, mandatory per-item indexes, admission receipts) rest on a gas falsifier that names a venue profile nobody has defined and a gas schedule Stage A itself says it took "from memory", while the Arcade README still states the falsified mirror-kill beat as the product promise.

Nothing here blocks a guest-read + basic-write File Browser MVP on Sepolia. Three things bind the freeze path (F1, F2, F3) and two bind the Arcade product framing (F8, F10).
