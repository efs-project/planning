## 0. Headline

The concept algebra is the same in all six documents: a Record is author-neutral exact typed content; an Occurrence is `(EnvelopeId, leafIndex)`; admission is Realm-qualified; a Binding is one Principal's CAS-guarded answer at one position; a Lens/ResolutionPlan resolves a position over Principals with honest non-answers. Nobody contradicts that. What is strained is everything one level down, and it is strained in one direction: **the MVP write journey and the Files proposal are specified against Stage A B0 names and shapes (`BindingSet/1`, `AdmissionIntent/1` with `leafMask`/`expectedRevisions`, `ChunkTree/1`, `TypeSchemaId`) that the declared spine (`system-constitution.md`, `core-architecture-candidate.md`) either does not name or defines differently, while the README-designated "current Type-system proposal" uses a third vocabulary (`TypeRevisionId`) that neither Files nor B0 uses.** One index the constitution, the 2026-07-22 owner ruling, the Files proposal and the MVP all require (`BindingScope`) exists in no Core document, is absent from Stage A's own gap register, and by Files' own rule can only be added at Realm genesis. Zero of Stage A's sixteen spine edits have been applied (git: no commit to the constitution, candidate, kickoff, or rulings ledger since the 2026-08-13 import). The spine is therefore behind both its engineering corpus and its product consumer, and a reader who starts at the spine cannot find the objects the MVP needs.

Nothing here is unsound at the concept level and every gap is labelled by at least one side, which is why the verdict is *strained* rather than *broken*. But an MVP write path cannot be built without five engineering decisions being written down first (§6), and one of them (`BindingScope` at genesis) is genesis-or-never for whatever Realm the MVP mints.

**Correction to the Phase 1 maps (verify-not-inherit).** `R1-efsv2-spine.md` F4 and `R2` F1 report "two `RecordId` preimages" between the candidate (`H(domain, typeSchemaId, canonicalBody)`, `core-architecture-candidate.md:115`) and the layered doc (`H(DOM_RECORD, TypeRevisionId, H(canonicalBody))`, `layered-type-system-and-data-abi.md:380`). That is a misreading. B0's exact form is `keccak256(abi.encode(DOM_RECORD, typeSchemaId, keccak256(canonicalBody)))` (`chapters/b0-encoding-and-ids.md:988`, table row 925) under SR-1's rule that "any variable-length or structured component enters as its own `keccak256` hash" (`b0-overview.md:75-77`); the candidate's line is the same formula written as shorthand, and `b0-overview.md:39-40` uses the identical shorthand. The nested body hash is *not* a mismatch. The only real difference between the two formulas is the Type-identity input (`typeSchemaId` vs `TypeRevisionId`), which is finding F1/F2 below.
