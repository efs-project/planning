## 3. Lane question 2 — Lens / ResolutionPlan vs owner target and V2-E2

Numbers (lens §2 table lines 67-81; §3.2; §9 table lines 855-867): `MAX_PLAN_ENTRIES_CORE = 64` [PROPOSAL], `MAX_PLAN_ENTRIES_CLIENT = 256` [HYPOTHESIS]; frame `96 + 64·N` bytes, max 4,192 (canonical body 4,194); entry = `bytes32 principalId` + `tier u16` + `entryFlags u16` + `minAuthFloor u64` + 20 reserved bytes; probe cost 1 cold SLOAD absent / 2 present; worst cold resolve N=64 ≈ 551.1k gas ≈ 3.28% of 16,777,216 (all-absent ≈ 416.7k); warm ≈ 31.1k; five worst-case resolves ≈ 2.755M; a naive 128-item × 55-principal directory page ≈ 29.5M "exceeds the cap and is not promised" (lines 872-876). Against V2-E2 (`Designs/efsv2/owner-decision-inbox.md` lines 26-31: "Plans of 1, 8, 32, and 64 Principals … first/last/absent/conflict/unknown, cold and warm reads, and risk-bearer policy"): FX-LENS matches the grid exactly (harness §2.5 lines 966-1018: 12 plans × 7 outcome classes, cold then warm, LENS-NEG-1). Against owner direction 8 (`Designs/web-client-os/README.md` lines 69-71: "64 Principal entries if measurement supports it. Multiple controller keys do not consume multiple Lens positions; key authorization belongs inside Principal verification"): the **number** agrees; the **unit** does not.

**Is a Principal entry a Principal or a key?** In B0 an entry is a `PrincipalId`, and a B0 `PrincipalId` is derived from one account/key descriptor: `keccak256(abi.encode(DOM_PRINCIPAL, uint256(kind), keccak256(descriptorBytes)))`, kinds `EOA_SECP256K1 | CONTRACT_ERC1271 | KEY_P256 | KEY_RSA` (ovw SR-14 lines 397-404; `chapters/b0-principal-authority.md` lines 1462-1465, worked example line 249-250 = one address). Managed / multi-key Principals are the deferred graduation seam (lens §6.5 `minAuthFloor` MBZ; traceability C-AA-4 DEFERRED; STATUS G-2). So for an EOA/passkey user **every controller key is its own entry**: James's own example (three controller keys, direction 7) consumes 3 of 64; only an ERC-1271 contract account collapses keys. The 15–55 "design center" lens §3.4 cites from `lens-spec.md` §9 (line 97) explicitly assumes "KEL collapses keys" — an assumption B0 does not provide. FX-LENS's 64 plan principals are 56 EOA + 4 P256 + 2 RSA + 2 ERC-1271 (harness line 968-970), i.e. the benchmark never measures a multi-key Principal. See finding CF-R7b-3.
