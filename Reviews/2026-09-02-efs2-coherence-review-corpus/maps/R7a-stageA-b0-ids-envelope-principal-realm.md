## 1. Standing and headline

**Standing of the set.** `Reviews/README.md` line 84 indexes the corpus as "completed specification/evidence Stage A … No proposal was applied to `Designs/efsv2/`; protocol adoption/freeze and all Stage B bytes … remain unclaimed." `STATUS.md` lines 3, 13-16 say "done at the specification/evidence level; proposal-only; Stage B unrun". Every chapter's first line reads "Stage A chapter — post-red-team revision; not landed, adopts nothing." Each chapter is tagged `#status/draft`. Per the brief's layering rule this is **a current proposal corpus with evidence standing, not a ruling and not a design-set doc**: the efsv2 spine (`system-constitution.md`, `core-architecture-candidate.md`, `owner-rulings.md`, `owner-decision-inbox.md`) outranks it, and `hierarchical-files-and-folders.md` line 6 declares a dependency on "the Stage A B0 candidate". Maturity: **draft-reviewed** (red-teamed and repaired to tip `6ea657e`, 2026-08-13; five same-day commits in `git log`), but nothing has been built or measured.

**Headline.** The four chapters plus the overview describe one internally consistent object model with an unusually disciplined ID family (SR-1/SR-14/SR-16), exact EIP-712 digests, a four-state occurrence lifecycle, a versioned authority verifier, and a Realm identity that is recomputable from state. Cross-chapter residue is small and mostly cosmetic (§3). The real weaknesses are not contradictions but *unowned holes on the MVP path*: the Realm policy hook has no grammar, `ERC1271_VERIFY_GAS` is an unmintable placeholder that blocks `RealmId` computation, `originRef` bytes for contract accounts are still marked "dependsOn Lane 4" after Lane 4 closed, no deployment/Realm-churn ceremony exists, and the gas model (`G_SSTORE_NEW ≈ 22,100`, `c_occ ≤ 90k`) never mentions EIP-8037. The Principal surface matches the web-client-os direction only for single-key Principals; the direction's multi-controller `JamesCarnley.eth` example needs the managed-Principal seam B0 explicitly defers. Every Files write (a `BindingSet` leaf) needs an explicit signed `AdmissionIntent` in addition to the envelope signature, so the write-capable File Browser is a two-signature product under B0 — a cost none of the chapters states.

## 4. Lane question 2 — what Realm means, bootstrap, reconstruction, "confirmed then unreadable"

**Realm =** one deployed atomic Core contract on one chain (`b0-authorship-envelope.md` §0 line 58: "one deployed Core contract IS one Realm"), identified by `RealmId = H(DOM_REALM, ChainRef/1, coreAddress, profileId, genesisCommitment)` (realm §2.2). `profileId` names the semantics contract (protocol 0.0 + the whole Codex byte artifact, realm §2.3); `genesisCommitment` names the deployment (`deployCodehash`, `deployBlock`, `codexConstantsHash`, `initConfigHash`, realm §2.4). Admission rules are the Codex (identical in every Realm) plus the revisioned `policyCommitment` (realm §5.5 "Policy hook"). The descriptor (§2.1) is *not* identity — "RealmId is the identity and is recomputable from section A alone" (line 136); section C (`rpcUrls`, `displayName`) is untrusted.

**Bootstrap of a fresh L3** (realm §2.4 lines 240-243, §7.1): deploy Core directly (kind NONE) or behind a UUPS-style proxy (kinds 1-3), call `initialize(InitConfig/1)`; Core reads `block.chainid`, computes and stores `genesisCommitment` and `RealmId`, writes revision 1 (§2.5 lines 272-275). A client then runs C-1..C-7 (§3 lines 302-343). The trust root is "possession of the right RealmId … obtaining it out-of-band is above Core" (lines 350-354). **Undefined:** who deploys/initializes (no deployer identity in `InitConfig/1`), how the RealmId reaches a client, the policy parameter set (see F4), and what a Realm operator's "adopting the qualifying assumptions" (spine-edit A2) means operationally.

**Independent reconstruction** needs only: `genesisFacts()`, `codexConstants()`, `revisionAt`/`authorityTransitionAt`, `envelopeIdByOrdinal` + `getEnvelopeBytes`, `getPrincipalRecord`, `admissionAt`/`admissionBatchAt`/`admissionBatchIntentLane`, `getRecord`, the Type cache, `preWithdrawalEvidenceAt`, index and Binding point reads (W-0..W-10, realm §8.1). No logs, no archive, no EFS service (QR-6). Reconstruction is "at the basis the RPC serves" only (§8.2 honesty note, lines 1821-1824).

**Does this answer "confirmed, then unreadable"?** At the Core-state layer, *yes, structurally*: full-body spine (owner-ruled), reversible two-word log, `envelopeIdByOrdinal` enumeration, C-6 semantic probe, and the encoding chapter's rule that "every write-form vector pairs with a read-back decode vector (the dominant historical EFS bug shape is writes that confirm then never read back)" (encoding §10 lines 1300-1303). Three things stay open: (a) the **main envelope witness is never stored** (authorship §4.3 lines 766-769; realm W-3 "Do not claim to replay the unstored main witness"), so a second implementation reconstructs *that Core said an author signed*, not *that the author signed* — finding F8; (b) reconstruction of a past snapshot needs archive state (not promised); (c) the layer where v1 actually failed — off-chain bytes/locators, the 67 non-canonical hashes, one-node pins — is the content-locators chapter (not this lane) and the evidence round's ~18.2-day blob window is not addressed by these four chapters at all (they only require `Locator/ByteDigest` for large content, authorship §2.5 lines 473-476).

## 5. Lane question 3 — the Principal surface vs direction 7 and V2-E1

**B0 surface** (principal §2, §3, §6): uniform `bytes32 PrincipalId` everywhere (AUTH-INV-5); the only Principal kind that exists is the intrinsic zero-setup `AccountPrincipal/1` — exactly one key or one contract account per PrincipalId; EOA and raw keys are chain-independent, ERC-1271 accounts are chain-qualified via `originRef` (§2.3); EIP-7702 delegation is observed into `delegateOrZero` and never changes identity (§4); the descriptor is persisted on first admission as `PrincipalRecord` (§2.7) and the exact `(kind, verifierVersion, witnessProfile, basisBlock, delegateOrZero[, codehash])` is persisted per accepting call (§3.5). **There is no mutable controller**: "an ungraduated `AccountPrincipal` has **no rotation** — its authority reference is immutable, so key theft means permanent capture of that PrincipalId's future writes" (§6.3 lines 1308-1314). Managed Principals are a reserved seam (`graduatePrincipal` "RESERVED (not in B0)", §6.1 line 1219) whose design is "out of Stage A scope" (§6 line 1197) and whose recovery boundary is open gap G-2 (`STATUS.md` line 64).

**Against V2-E1** (`Designs/efsv2/owner-decision-inbox.md` lines 17-24): the inbox asks for "one uniform PrincipalId … with Realm-qualified EOA and smart accounts represented as zero-setup account Principals" versus "a tagged `Account | Principal` author reference" and says "James's preference is one semantic Principal surface; it is not frozen until the comparison proves it honest and simpler." B0 implements exactly the uniform arm, labels it `[PROPOSAL — B0 axis-2 pin; V2-E1 open]` (`traceability.md` line 137), and supplies the tagged arm as bakeoff cell F2 with decision results D1-D5 (principal §7). D1-D4 "resolve in uniform's favor by construction/inspection"; D5 (gas) awaits the harness. **Match: yes.** The 2026-08-12 ruling (`owner-rulings.md` lines 216-219) lists this as "Open, not ruled", and `Open-Decisions.md` line 47 still shows V2-E1 waiting on evidence.

**Against web-client-os direction 7 and 8** (`Designs/web-client-os/README.md` lines 61-71, recorded 2026-08-14→23, i.e. *after* Stage A): "one uniform `PrincipalId` surface" — matches. "every operation still names and historically verifies its actual signer descriptor and, when applicable, the account it used" — matches (`PrincipalRecord` + `AuthorityBasisWord.delegateOrZero`/codehash). **"A Principal may have a mutable default/main controller account … `JamesCarnley.eth` may have three controller keys"** and direction 8 **"Multiple controller keys do not consume multiple Lens positions; key authorization belongs inside Principal verification"** — **does not match B0**: in B0 three keys are three `PrincipalId`s and three Lens entries; the only mechanism that makes "controller keys inside Principal verification" true is the deferred managed Principal. The README restates this at lines 318-324 ("Its public action surface uses `PrincipalId`, while a mutable default controller account … remain separate … Lens entries are Principals rather than underlying controller keys"). Neither the V2-E1 inbox entry nor the B0 chapters were reconciled with this later direction. Finding F1.
