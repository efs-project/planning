## 1. Headline

This lane is the *reason trail* of EFS 2.0, not its current design. Five of the six documents are July artefacts (2026-07-07 → 2026-07-23) that the 2026-08-12 greenfield reset explicitly demoted to evidence; the sixth (`README.md`) is the current phone entry point. The ledger and the human overview received an 08-12 correction banner; `ethereum-first-efs-and-os.md`, `solana.md`, `playable-archive-requirements.md` and `client-os-pressure-report.md` did not, and none of the six is tagged with the vault's own `#status/superseded` state. The set is cohesive *internally* (the July docs cross-reference each other precisely) but is now cut off from the current spine: the constitution cites none of the ledger's stable R-/D- identifiers, the README's evidence map omits four of the six, and the only live pointers into them come from historical `clientv2/` and the (held) Arcade set. The real risk is not that a newcomer reads a wrong mechanism — the banners handle that — it is that requirements which were never *consciously* dropped (century preservation, durability baseline, admission time, count semantics, the A-1 chain-persistence assumption now contradicted by evidence) have silently lost their owner.
