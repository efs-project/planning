Every one of them is `historical` or `evidence-only` per its own status line and per `Designs/efsv2/README.md` §"Evidence map" (which lists only the *outputs* of three of them: `[[lens-spec]]`/`[[lens-pass-synthesis]]`, `[[kel]]`, `[[onchain-completeness]]`). What is *not* clean: (a) the two 2026-07-10 reviews (century storage; cypherpunk-OS coherence audit) were never registered anywhere — zero backlinks from any `Designs/` doc, zero hits in the Stage A carry-in register, not indexed in `Reviews/README.md`; (b) three 2026-07-10/07-16 owner rulings that are *assumptions*, not mechanisms ("chains don't die", "KEL — design it", "passkey-sync default recovery") still stand in the ledger while the greenfield spine targets ephemeral fresh L3s and ships account Principals with no rotation or recovery; (c) the reset reopened contract-account (ERC-1271) authorship that three reviews ruled out of portable authorship, and dropped the KEL review's first-use future-control commitment, so every MVP identity is an "uncommitted legacy identity" with what the KEL review called an "irreducible thief race".
