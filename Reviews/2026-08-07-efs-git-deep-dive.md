## 1. Executive verdict, in plain language

**Git-backed Markdown/wiki workspaces on EFS are viable, and they — not a GitHub replacement — are the right first product.** The pass was told to earn or reject that conclusion; it is earned three ways on the supply side: the FS pass's draft canon routes public collaboration through revision-DAGs + curation (exactly what Git is — that arm is **held decision Q3**, so this pass adds a concrete workload of evidence toward Q3A rather than treating it as ruled); every scale risk (browser Git, admission cost, container size) is smallest at wiki scale while every differentiator (identity, authority, ref history, proposals, anonymous reads, exit) is fully exercised; and the strongest single gap in the surveyed field is precisely a wiki gap — no git-backed wiki anywhere has portable proposals ("propose an edit" on GitHub wikis is collaborator-or-nothing), and Wikipedia's social layer has never had a credibly neutral substrate under it. The honesty the adversarial pass added: those three legs are all *supply-side*. Who edits this wiki, why, and how it cold-starts — the Everipedia/NIP-54 lesson that community, not substrate, is what makes wikis succeed — is a genuine open product question the owner packet carries rather than buries, and the proposer-funding contradiction (§5) is its sharpest technical edge.

**The central technical finding:** the one mechanism this workload genuinely needs that nothing ships — a replay-resistant, policy-epoch-bound, atomic multi-ref compare-and-swap with a defined public reading — is expressible **with zero kernel changes**, as a typed read-time fold (`GIT-REF/1`) over ordinary claim batches sequenced by admission ordinals at one genesis-pinned authority home. The ingredients are all ordinary: transaction commitments and predecessor witnesses ride the claim bodies (`txnRoot`/`refCount` defeat subset-carrying relayers; `expectedPriorClaimId` + a one-shot rule defeat ABA and stale-envelope replay); the kernel's first-authoritative-admission rule makes re-carried claims no-ops; the lens family supplies the policy typing. Git CAS becomes a deterministic read rule, not an admission rule — admission confluence is preserved and nothing Git-shaped enters the kernel. Two limits are part of the finding, not caveats to it: fold-derived heads are **CAS-canonical** — ancestry ("was that ADVANCE actually fast-forward?") and byte availability live in Git objects, so they are verified deterministically by *object-bearing* readers above the fold, with violations flagged and fail-closed in default views; and the strongest grade is chain-connected — a G1 guest verifies the page they see, not that it is current. Radicle's 18-month signed-refs replay vulnerability (fixed exactly the way this design starts: predecessor binding), its silently-stalling canonical-ref quorum, and NIP-34's forgeable `created_at` ordering are three independent field demonstrations that a public total order is the ingredient everyone else is improvising.

**Everything else is reuse.** Stock Git supplies objects, packs, atomic push, CAS transactions, closure enumeration, fsck gates, bundles, and (the sleeper) bundle-URI serving — verified first-hand in the experiment set. Wikipedia runs on the same *class* of line-based 3-way merge Git uses (GNU diff3 there, xdiff here), so a sentence-per-line + normalized-CommonMark house style makes stock merge conflict at same-or-adjacent-sentence granularity (verified, E9b) with no semantic-merge invention — which the literature says produces silent corruption anyway. SWH proves objects + ref-snapshots rebuild serving infrastructure at 2 PB scale; SWHIDs mean EFS commits are ISO-standard citations for free.

## 2. What EFS should reuse vs uniquely build

## 3. The recommended boundary (candidate B of four)

Four architectures were developed and attacked ([candidate-architectures](./2026-08-07-efs-git-corpus/candidate-architectures.md)): **A** standard-Git-plus-generic-records (the mandated smallest option — necessary, insufficient: it is "GoE done right" and leaves the gateway a de facto authority); **B** the Git-workspace profile (recommended); **C** chain-maximal contract-enforced Git (rejected: gas-absurd or trust-theater, violates admission confluence, and GoE's own implemented-vs-designed gap is its field evidence); **D** Radicle-adoption (rejected as core: two identity systems, no durability/anonymous-read story, CRDT-governance bug class; mined for namespaces and the replay lesson — and its "deleted the chain and grew" arc is answered concretely, not waved at: B spends the chain only on the five things Heartwood demonstrably lacks).

The boundary in one table ([state-model](./2026-08-07-efs-git-corpus/state-model.md)):

- **Git canonical for:** file contents and per-file version history. EFS mints no per-commit version records (hypothesis H-3 survives, sharpened).
- **EFS canonical for:** ref transitions and their history (Git provably cannot keep this — experiment E6: force-push + GC permanently destroys displaced history; forges keep it by private convention), publish/accept provenance, page identity across renames, placement/availability evidence, policy and its epochs.
- **Derived, always rebuildable:** gateway bare repos, web views, indexes, LFS maps, pack layouts (E3).
- **Kernel: no new surface identified** — provisional until M2's two implementations and the cost fixtures run; every substrate need found so far is a rider on items already in front of James (P-1, E2, Q3, AMBIENT/1, `.efs-bundle`, conventions registry) — [freeze-impact](./2026-08-07-efs-git-corpus/freeze-impact.md).
- **Interop boundary, stated plainly:** anonymous stock clone/fetch is full-fidelity; stock `git push` lands as **proposal intake** (refs/for-style, honest within receive-pack's synchronous report-status); *direct authoritative publish requires an EFS-aware countersign path*. "Full authenticated stock-Git push to canonical refs" is not claimed in v1 — the trilemma has no honest third option.

## 4. The wiki, concretely

Edit/Preview/Save-draft/Propose/Publish/History/Compare/Restore map onto journal drafts, local commits, closure-then-claim publication, and revert-only restores, with the existing pending-ladder vocabulary unchanged ([wiki-and-collab](./2026-08-07-efs-git-corpus/wiki-and-collab.md)). Same-or-adjacent-sentence conflicts get a MediaWiki-TwoColConflict-class two-versions UI — budgeted as the hardest single UI; the Wikipedia <1 % contention figure is a lower bound here (offline sessions and fee-driven batching widen conflict windows — an M5 measurement, not an assumption). Canonical wiki branches prohibit force-push forever; Restore is a revert commit (H-10 confirmed). Two Wikipedia mechanisms are carried as day-one requirements: **lens-tier revision hiding** — with its parity gap stated plainly: masking governs default rendering only; the bytes remain permanently retrievable, so prevention (pre-publish PII scanning, accepted-head gating for risk namespaces) is part of the requirement, and nothing in EFS reproduces Wikipedia's *suppression* — and **accepted-head read-side selection** (pending-changes falls out of policy-derived refs for free). Reading without an account rides the existing guest ladder at G1 with the per-view honesty split (page bytes/authorship verified; head currency and history completeness are the endpoint's word until a chain-connected read); proposing without an account is **pseudonymous and permanent, never anonymous** — the house privacy rule, now in the workspace copy. The wiki blocks on AMBIENT/1 like every guest product and adds no new surface.

## 5. Largest unresolved risk

**The proposer-economics contradiction is the sharpest single risk**: self-paid proposals bound spam but are drive-by-fatal friction against Wikipedia's free anon edit; sponsored proposals restore onboarding but dissolve the spam bound into an undesigned sponsor-side defense. Unreconciled — a named unknown, falsifier, and acceptance-test parameter, not a solved property. Second: **the strongest grade of the whole design consumes P-1** (admission ordinals + receipts); if P-1 were rejected, ref history falls to venue-local/provider-attested rungs — candidate-A grades with honest labels: a working product, but the replay story that beats Radicle's incident class is what degrades. Third, economic: per-publish **admission cost** (E2 fixture) *and* **container-placement cost/latency** (outside E2's kernel scope — its own M1/M5 fixture) are both unpriced on the publish path. Fourth: the two-implementation determinism of `GIT-REF/1` — honestly a GATE/1-scale state machine, not a small fold — is undemonstrated until M2's vector suite, which is the first falsifier.

**GD-3 — SHA-1 posture.** Accept SHA-1 repos as the interop default (forges and browser engines still force it), with dual-digest head binding in every EFS ref/release statement (interior-graph SHA-1 risk remains detection-only via SHA-1DC — stated, not hidden) plus container-digest byte binding; the descriptor pins `objectFormat` at genesis and SHA-256 repos are supported where they exist; cross-format migration is a labeled successor operation, not v1 machinery. *(Rec: yes — E7 shows the formats still don't interoperate in transport; Git 3.0's SHA-256 default lands mid-flight and changes nothing here. **GD-4 — Canonical-branch rollback law** for `workspaceClass = wiki`: routine force never applicable; Restore = revert commits; the only escape is a disclosed policy-epoch recovery ceremony (quorum per plan — also the unbrick path for a placement mistake); displaced-closure retention on by default. Enforcement honesty ratified with it: the fold checks CAS, not ancestry — a lying ADVANCE lands on-chain but is flagged `ANCESTRY-VIOLATION` by every object-bearing verifier and fails closed in default Views. *(Rec: yes — H-10 as originally worded (ceremony, never routine) plus E6's demonstration that Git itself guarantees nothing.)*

**GD-5 — Adopt the day-one moderation split** for wiki serving: lens-tier revision hiding honored by default Views, swh-alter-pattern serving-plane quarantine per operator (public removed-objects feed, separate reasons channel, mirror discretion), curation never rewriting history. **Ratified knowingly:** this lever hides from default views only — the bytes remain permanently retrievable by any non-default View or carrier fetch; it is materially weaker than Wikipedia suppression, the erasure residual lands on serving operators, and the prevention half (PII scanning, accepted-head gating) ships with it. *(Rec: yes — routine per the Wikipedia evidence; must not be retrofitted. ## 7. What remains unknown

- **Proposer funding vs spam bound** — the design's sharpest open contradiction (§5): sponsor-side ceilings/deposits, a gateway-attested free tier at a degraded rung, or self-pay friction accepted; undesigned.
- `GIT-REF/1` cross-implementation determinism (M2 vectors decide) — at its honest GATE/1 scale.
- Real admission cost per publish at wiki cadence (E2 fixture) **and container-placement cost/latency on the publish path** (its own M1/M5 fixture).
- Publish→guest-visible latency budget (seconds-class acknowledgment vs minutes-class settlement; tied to E1 venue evidence).
- Real conflict rate under offline/batched editing (the Wikipedia <1 % figure is a lower bound; M5 measures).
- The wiki's demand side: target community and cold-start (the Everipedia/NIP-54 lesson).
- EthStorage/GoE production readiness (unchanged gates; whitelist mining, no retrieval SLA as of Mainnet Alpha).
- The stock-push credential ceremony's actual UX (P-G8's refs/for-style intake for all stock pushes + countersign flow needs a prototype; `proc-receive` derisks proposal intake, and direct-publish is EFS-client-only because receive-pack's synchronous report-status leaves no honest way to say "pending" on `refs/heads/*`).
- Whether an ed25519 actor suite is acceptable KEL surface (would let SSH keys be actor keys directly).
- Wiki-scale sharding thresholds and phone editing beyond the read-mostly envelope.
- Private repositories: explicitly out of v1; the metadata-honesty problem is unsolved and stays with the privacy pass.

## 8. What would kill this direction

1. The fold's determinism failing across two implementations (not patchable — the design's keystone).
1b. No proposer-funding design that simultaneously keeps drive-by proposing viable and spam bounded — the wiki's differentiator then narrows to trusted-editor workspaces (still real, much smaller claim).
2. P-1 rejected **and** provider-attested rungs judged insufficient for canonical wikis — the product then reduces to candidate A plus honest labels.
3. E2 pricing a batched wiki publish beyond sponsorable range with no acceptable degraded rung.
4. Browser-side sparse checkout+commit infeasible on current phones **and** users rejecting gateway-assisted editing (the browser lane's evidence currently says wiki-scale is comfortable, so this would require the evidence to be wrong).

Each is a falsifier to test, none is currently in evidence.
