# Grant Programs

Initial funder landscape for Ethereum File System. This is not exhaustive; it is the working shortlist and watchlist for grant strategy.

Last broad research pass: 2026-09-11. This is a non-exhaustive funding and
income sweep. The dated entry in [[research-log]] records coverage and gaps;
rows not named there retain their earlier evidence, not an implied fresh check.

## Fit thesis

EFS should not pitch itself as "another storage network." The strongest grant framing is:

> EFS is building open Ethereum-native infrastructure for portable paths, provenance, mirrors, and verifiable data references.

Use [[efs-grant-packet]] for the evidence boundary: public v1/Sepolia proof and
current v2 work are different. A funding opportunity does not adopt a backend,
product vertical, deployment or protocol decision.

That makes storage systems such as IPFS, Filecoin, Arweave, and future backends complements. EFS should track and verify where content lives, who attested it, which mirrors exist, and which lens/path currently points to it.

## Priority read

Newly scouted routes, checked 2026-09-11, are included in the existing tables;
[[funding-income]] explains paid work and personal runway options without
creating another proposal-status board.

Near-term qualification priorities:

- Arbitrum Open House: timely go/no-go on a small existing-project entry, not
  an assumed award or reason to redirect EFS's roadmap.
- Emergent Ventures: a compact individual application and actual development
  budget, without a chain-specific integration requirement.
- FUTO Fellows: meaningful development support if the Austin residency works;
  coordinate with the pending microgrant, whose follow-up is already sent.
- ESP Office Hours: identify the right EF team or future funding instrument.
- NLnet: resolve the European-dimension and material GenAI eligibility risks
  before investing in a proposal. Disclosure alone does not establish eligibility.
- Filecoin Open Grants or Sentient: qualify only against an actual user need
  and existing integration/AI evidence, not a speculative new product.
- Paid engineering and sponsorship options in [[funding-income]].

Later or conditional routes:

- GitHub Secure OSS: short evidence check, then confirm the next cohort.
- Sovereign Tech Fund / Resilience and Alpha-Omega: stronger adoption and
  downstream-dependency evidence first; not general prototype funding.
- Giveth: donor/profile route now, a relevant matching round only when announced.
- Gitcoin, Base/Optimism, The Graph, Ocean, IPFS, Sia and FIL PGF: verify a new
  eligible intake/RFP before significant application work.

Precedent-mining priority:

1. Filecoin GitHub issues - best source for full public proposal structure.
2. NLnet grantee-published applications - best source for compact technical/budget framing.
3. Octant, Gitcoin, Giveth, Optimism, Questbook-style ecosystems - best public profile/results data for community and impact framing.
4. OTF, Sovereign Tech, GitHub Secure Open Source Fund, Alpha-Omega - best for criteria, reports, and funded-project patterns, but not full applications.
