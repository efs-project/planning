## Fellowships and individual support

**FUTO Fellows:** up to USD 40k plus three months of housing, airfare and
workspace in Austin. Rolling individual selection; international applicants
welcome. First check availability, project ownership/publication terms and
how any award would interact with the pending microgrant. No response or
payout schedule is promised. [Official program](https://futo.tech/grants/fellows).

**Emergent Ventures:** global individual applications, age 13+, no standard
award range. Its form asks for a self-contained narrative of at most 1,500
words and a basic budget; PDF attachments are not accepted. It says responses
are typically within a week, which is not a promise of acceptance or payment.
The strongest EFS hypothesis is a specific public-data exit/verification
deliverable and the development time needed to ship it.
[Application](https://mercatus.tfaforms.net/5099527).

## Offers to test with potential buyers

These scopes and prices are unvalidated starting hypotheses, not market
quotes, agreed rates or guaranteed demand. Prefer work that produces reusable
open-source examples while preserving EFS's existing rights and direction.

- **Transaction-state integration review:** 2-3 days, proposed USD 1k-2k.
  Buyer hypothesis: wallet/escrow teams. Deliver a reproduction of ambiguous
  transaction states, recovery cases and a prioritized engineering report.
- **Portable-record verifier prototype:** 4-6 days, proposed USD 2k-4k.
  Buyer hypothesis: attestation, credential or impact-reporting teams. Deliver
  one portable export, independent verification and explicit failure cases.
- **Ethereum SDK examples and testing sprint:** 3-5 days, proposed USD 1.5k-3k.
  Buyer hypothesis: wallet, RPC and developer-tool teams. Deliver a bounded
  integration example, reproducible tests and maintenance handoff.

Sell these as engineering deliverables. EFS prototype work alone is not a
professional security-audit credential or proof of mainnet readiness. Scope
production access, continuing support and IP rights explicitly before a job.
No prospects have been contacted and no demand is validated by this note.

## Recurring sponsorship and payment channels

**GitHub Sponsors:** an individual maintainer can set sponsorship amounts in
a supported region, including the US. Personal-account sponsorships have no
GitHub fee; organization-funded sponsorships can incur up to 6%. The terms
include a 60-day wait after the first sponsorship before the initial payment.
Useful once there are identified supporters; creating a page does not create
income. [Eligibility and fees](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/getting-started-with-github-sponsors/about-github-sponsors),
[payout terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-sponsors-additional-terms).

**Open Source Collective:** a possible fiscal host for a qualifying openly
licensed project in organizational repositories. Published host fee is 10%
plus processing. Funded project work can pay James through documented invoices;
it is not an unrestricted personal grant. Useful when a funder needs an
organizational payment route. [Acceptance](https://docs.oscollective.org/interested-in-joining-osc/acceptance-criteria),
[fees](https://docs.oscollective.org/welcome-and-introduction-to-osc/fees),
[application](https://docs.oscollective.org/how-to-apply).

**Drips:** repository funding and maintainer splits in ERC-20 tokens; no
protocol fee, but gas applies. Inspect any existing unclaimed support before
considering setup; none was checked in this sweep. New streams do not imply
instant withdrawal or predictable fiat revenue.
[Claiming](https://docs.drips.network/get-support/claim-your-repository/),
[FAQ](https://docs.drips.network/faq/).

Avoid counting cloud credits, matching pools, token headline values or
unawarded grants as money available for living costs.
