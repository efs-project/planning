## 1. The headline results

**JR-1 — The two-grade authority hypothesis is validated — as a theorem about ONE axis, with a binding presentation rule.** Bare/portable authorship works on any chain with zero setup (`PORTABLE-EVIDENCE`); the strong grade (`AUTHORITY-ADMITTED`) exists exactly when admission is co-ordered with KEL state at an authority home — that is what rejecting a stolen-key backdated record structurally requires; portable evidence alone can never promise it ([authority-model §1](../../Reviews/2026-07-25-joined-fs-pass-corpus/authority-model.md)). But a read result is a **tuple** — (authorization, existence bound, freshness/basis, availability, slot state, completeness) — and the two grades are the floor/ceiling of the *authorization* axis only. **The F-15 rule is binding: no packet, product copy, or UI may compress the tuple to "two grades = two labels"** — a single green checkmark recreates the collapsed-Boolean failure of [[human-overview#7. The seams that must be closed|seam 7]]. `EVIDENCE-ORDERED@N` (venue-ordered evidence without authority admission) is a read-vocabulary label, not a third authorization tier.

**JR-2 — The strong grade's honest price is admission timing, and it is now priced.** Two repairs harden the theorem's edges: (a) **promotion across a revocation** (D-1): promotion requires live authorization at promotion time; a pre-revocation `EVIDENCE-ORDERED@N` ordinal refutes *dating* claims forever but never upgrades *authorization* — product copy says "promote promptly"; (b) **revocation-censorship** (D-2): the theft window is bounded by *revocation-inclusion latency under adversarial ordering*, so **force-inclusion latency is a security parameter of an acceptable authority home** — now an [[owner-decision-inbox]] E1 rider (P-5r2), with fixed grant expiries as the backstop.

**JR-3 — The L1 pointer is designed, judged, and shelved conditionally.** Nothing in the MUST-requirement set forces it ([use-cases](../../Reviews/2026-07-25-joined-fs-pass-corpus/use-cases.md) register, independently attacked and standing). Two corrections from the red team change the pricing, not the verdict: discovery is NOT "inside the ID" — a genesis-committed home is *checkable from* the 32-byte principal word, never *extractable* (D-3), so discovery bootstrap is a digest-checkable **hint convention** (unforgeable, since every hint verifies against the word); and the pointer **cannot deliver censorship escape** (D-4) — re-homing starts with an admission at the censoring home, so a pointer never exceeds the source home's cooperation. The pointer-theft theorem and its dual carry: *a pointer adds discovery, never authority security.* Disposition is the owner's P-5 (recommended arm: no re-home promise in v2; the pointer stays a designed shelf candidate).

**JR-4 — The residual EFS identity layer shrank, and its boundary is now exact.** Mid-2026 account abstraction (7702 live since Pectra, 4337 mature, ERC-7913 verifiers, EIP-7951 P-256 live since Fusaka 2025-12) provides **current execution authority** — rotation, passkeys, sessions, recovery ceremonies, thresholds — and EFS consumes all of it. What no account mechanism provides is **authorization-as-durable-evidence**: portable, historically-verifiable, realm-independent proof that a signature was authorized *at the time*, surviving rotation and state change. That gap is the residual: **R1–R6 with R1's home-binding field held parametric** (D-6: `authorityHomeRef` sits inside the genesis preimage, so freezing R1 as written would pre-answer the topology — the field's presence/meaning/mutability waits for P-5). ERC-1271/6492 remain banned from canonical record authority; stateless 7913-shaped verifiers are consumable as pinned implementations. The [aa-inversion §5](../../Reviews/2026-07-25-joined-fs-pass-corpus/aa-inversion.md) section map is the blessed [[kel]] recut work order.

**JR-5 — The filesystem has one absence rule, one result model, and a mount-honest profile split.** Adopted system-wide ([critic §2.1](../../Reviews/2026-07-25-joined-fs-pass-corpus/critic.md)): `ABSENT_PROVEN` has exactly **four sources** — own-node total-state read; verified state proof to positive closure; venue-committed bundle closure manifest; **a signed closed-realm/bundle completeness manifest at signer-trust grade** — and every native not-found is grade-carrying; `UNKNOWN` never maps to not-found. The red team proved the ordinary-user boundary was the weak spot (a hosted-RPC live mount cannot honestly emit `ENOENT`; `ls -l` ghosts): the repair is the **P-16 profile split** — snapshot/bundle-with-closure-manifest as the *ordinary-app* mount profile, own-node live mounts first-class, hosted-RPC live as a diagnostic surface — plus the handle-scoped `getattr` adapter obligation (D-8).

**JR-6 — FS-LENS/1 is the replacement seed for the reopened [[read-lens-spec]]** — first chapter only. Blessed: the ordered-principal FS profile as the simplest compiled policy of the typed-lens model; the one-basis invariant (point lookup and page enumeration agree at one basis); the seam-6 equivocation half **closed and attack-verified**; the authority-gated plain-name rule (D-10: across principals, higher lens tier takes the plain name — a cross-author squatter cannot evict a higher-authority file); the five-part view identity (realm+code basis, lens version, evidence basis, completeness policy, evaluation time); the portable-name projection. Superseded: the flat universal author list, checkpoint-grounded absence, the global same-order equivocation rule, MUST-pull-home via the `home` row (advisory-only under every topology). GATE/package/discovery/moderation profiles remain **unowned** — the dedicated lens pass (G-A) is the next foundation move.

**JR-7 — Large on-chain files are first-class, and T3 dissolved as vocabulary.** "The manifest is the generation": size, codec, content commitment, chunk geometry, mirror set, and byte basis resolve as one signed `FileManifest` generation; ruling 6's first-class file is the **state tier** (EFSBytes — contract-readable in bounded gas, passing the adopted item-16 test); the calldata rail stays DA-tier/@EPHEMERAL exactly as ruled; the tier-vocabulary honesty rule (P-20) enforces that nothing lets DA-tier or mirror bytes read as "on-chain." Two red-team repairs adopted as design center: **offset/length-committing chunk leaves** (D-14 — without them, mirror-tier files stream but cannot offer authenticated random access) and **geometry-bound store identity** (D-15 — closes a permissionless store-creation griefing hole). The chunk-authorization-free invariant stands (`submitChunk` never checks authority epochs).

**JR-8 — The chain-free/local mode is a designed product ladder with an honest loss list.** Local-sovereign → network-replicated → provider-attested → witnessed → chain-authoritative rungs, each labeled; the **cannot-do ledger** is the register's L1–L10 (freshness, completeness/absence, canonical order, strong-grade backdating rejection, contract composability, permissionless durability, neutral admission, discovery, live counts, permissionless completion) **plus the ledger's added schema-as-enforcement row**, each with its cheapest upgrade ([local-mode §6](../../Reviews/2026-07-25-joined-fs-pass-corpus/local-mode.md)). Promotion preserves identity **via the designed in-place EOA→KEL upgrade path** (the identity word survives); a fresh-digest principal is a new namespace + signed redirect — said loudly (D-11). Signed-head honesty: two conflicting heads convict only under the durable-counter discipline (D-13); a provider hiding the newest head is structurally detectable only by witnessing (P-13/P-14).

**JR-9 — Chains-as-drives holds, with one free capability discovered.** The per-chain model is designed ([filesystem-core §2](../../Reviews/2026-07-25-joined-fs-pass-corpus/filesystem-core.md)); the merged cross-chain view's hard parts are named and the one non-client-policy part is exactly the held P-5 decision surface. D-5's correction: rollup pairs sharing L1 settlement have a **verifiable coarse cross-realm existence/freshness bound** (batch-commitment ordering) — a free capability, not machinery; sovereign pairs stay heuristic-only. The FS-vs-OS-venue hypothesis largely dissolves technically (co-residency rule, P-4); the residual is product acceptance.

**JR-10 — Scale and scope honesty.** No requirement forces cross-chain machinery at MUST grade; high-frequency telemetry classes (10^8/day) surfaced as an explicit scope decision (P-21) instead of silent sizing; history-amplification budgets (live-small/history-large folders) promoted to a kernel index-shape obligation (R-QC8) and the mount-budget ⇄ current-live-index coupling is the sharpest new [[owner-decision-inbox]] E2 input.
