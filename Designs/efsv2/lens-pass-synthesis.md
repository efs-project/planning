## 1. The headline results

**LN-1 — The typed compiled-policy model survived full-family instantiation.** `EvidenceGraph + BasisVector + EffectiveLens + Context → ResolvedView + ViewReceipt` ([2026-07-11 review](../../Reviews/2026-07-11-efsv2-lens-architecture-and-scale-review.md)) is design center: two passes and every consumer in the register compiled inside the grammar without extending it. Its constitutional ratification is now askable (**LP-1** = D-13/N2c revalidated). The review's §18.1 fifteen decisions are dispositioned one-by-one in [critic §3.2](../../Reviews/2026-07-25-lens-pass-corpus/critic.md): fourteen design-center, one changed (identical-plan-semantics, now conditional on LR-2/LR-3). (Separately, the §21 packet's identity-encoding item is settled — no lossy encoding anywhere; ordinals an E2 shelf option, default NO.)

**LN-2 — "Lens" stops being nine things: the naming family.** **Lens** (the end-user read-view — the only word users see) · **View** (a saved, linkable lens + location + presentation + completeness config that can never contain trust) · **Starter Pack** · **Follow** (discovery only) · **Channel** (subscription pointer) · **Labeler** + **Action Map** (who says what vs what you do about it) · **Roster** (the shared trust-list primitive: ordered principal entries with tiers) · **Plan** (Roster + combiner + purpose + scope + modes + advisory rules = the executable contract-tier slice) · **GATE** (the contract/install trust function, deliberately machine-cold). The Roster/Plan split is [critic LR-1 §4](../../Reviews/2026-07-25-lens-pass-corpus/critic.md) — forced by the pass's own lanes using "Roster" for two objects. "Lens" never appears in a contract ABI. Ratification = **LP-3**.

**LN-3 — The contract tier: one artifact, self-verifying by construction, zero Etched state.** **LR-1:** `PlanV1` is one packed, fixed-width, offset-free byte layout; no contract ever parses CBOR (the deterministic-CBOR profile stays client-side); storage is **immutable code at a content-derived address** (SSTORE2-shape, `CREATE2`-derived from `planId`), read by `EXTCODECOPY` — so the plan store is not a trusted party and *address derivation is the verification* (closing the pass's sharpest no-Graph leak, a trusted plan registry). Sub-plan proofs are deleted (a proof identifies *which plan*, never which subset). The CORE combiner whitelist is `EXACT` / `PRIORITY_FIRST_PRESENT` / `THRESHOLD` + advisory point-deny; the CORE per-plan cap (candidate 64) and the client compile ceiling (E6's candidate 256) are **two constants doing two jobs** — conflating them was the `MAX_LENSES = 20` mistake. **LR-2:** authority status is **claim-conditional** — evaluated only for principals holding a PRESENT claim at the position, at or above the winner (this alone restores identical direct/roster semantics and deletes the gate-wedging lever). **LR-3:** every resolution **direct-probes every entry ranked strictly above the selected winner** — the claimant roster becomes a pure planning hint whose completeness is not a correctness dependency, and the challenge-window recheck becomes decision-scoped (repairing the adopted item-F pattern, which as first instantiated had no sound form at busy positions).

**LN-4 — The on-chain promise gained physics.** EIP-7825's 16,777,216 tx cap is **live** (Fusaka, 2025-12-03 — [research §3](../../Reviews/2026-07-25-lens-pass-corpus/research.md), VERIFIED): a 128-item × 55-principal naive directory page (~29.5M) is now *permanently impossible* in any transaction on a 7825 chain. The 2026-07-11 "should not be promised" is now "cannot be delivered." Bounded candidates + exact venue-local points + fixed-basis client materialization as the core promise is askable (**LP-2** = held D-9 restated). Gas honesty: the critic independently recomputed the lanes' figures (floors ~3× understated; the roster lever is ~4–7× cold single-point, decaying with winner rank — not ~10×); every number is E2 input, not a freeze claim.

**LN-5 — The profile family completes the book FS-LENS/1 opened.** Each profile is a compiled-policy instance with a closed vocabulary and fail-to-mount on excess: **GATE/1** (TUF-shaped: owner-pinned, fail-closed non-configurable, STOP on relinquish, closed enumerated authorities, no discovery influence — hardened by the red team with `policyMaxAge` against TUF-freeze, closure-wide advisory evaluation against transitive-dependency misses, closed action tables with unknown label values mapping to NONE, and ceremony-time **selection re-derivation** against presentation-layer rollback, the pass's sharpest product finding); **ADVISORY/1** (subtract-after-resolve carried; **source-level relinquish** — a dead deny feed grades `UNEVALUATED`, never clean); **DISCOVERY/1** (structurally authority-free); five named collaboration read-hooks that feed held Q3 without answering it. Seam 19 is discharged into GATE/1.

**LN-6 — One read-result model: the 6+1 axes + the acceptance matrix.** The joined pass's six-part tuple (F-15) + the seventh policy-outcome axis (advisory), with `UNEVALUATED(reason)` added, absence carrying **rule-coverage provenance** (incl. `AMBIENT_BASELINE_ONLY`), and composite completeness = **minimum over members** (`INCOMPLETE_BUDGET` dominates `FINAL`). Consumers declare an `AcceptanceMatrixV1` (hashed, pinned; no wildcards on authorization/existence/completeness; `onReject: FAIL_CLOSED` only) — the bitmask alternative is deleted. All staleness/basis-age display is clock-domain-qualified.

**LN-7 — Composition honesty, corrected before it could calcify.** CH-1 ("no composition can produce false absence") was **false as written** — a higher-priority covering import *replaces* an applicable rule rather than adding to it. Restated relative-to-plan with three structural guards: **the reserved user band** (the user's directly-authored rules outrank all imports, unconditionally), **displacement diffs** (an import that takes a policy key from a rule with a different roster renders as "removes N sources", never as a coverage note), and coverage-on-the-result. CH-2 (no false equivocation through composition) survived attack. The absence rule's source 3 gains its missing precondition: **a closure manifest may commit only scopes enumerated to `FINAL`** — budget-incomplete scopes commit as `PARTIAL(cursor)` and no consumer derives absence inside one (closing the pass's sharpest safety finding: `INCOMPLETE_BUDGET` laundered into signed durable false absence).

**LN-8 — Views, links, and the guest are designed, with a non-suppressible floor.** A **View** can never contain trust (decoder-enforced); hinted views are **offered, never applied**, through one uniform network-indistinguishable path; URL params **may narrow, pin, or decorate — never relocate, never loosen**; `?lenses=`/`?deny=` principal arrays are deleted from the link grammar (trust-adjacent keys ride the fragment); citations split — an object/claim citation carries **no policy reference by default**, a view citation carries the disclosure preview (**LP-9**). The **NS-1…NS-11 floor** (grades/basis, staleness, foreign-view banner + one-tap escape, advisory counts, conflict markers, authorship boundaries, look-alike warnings carrying their own completeness, guest state, degradation notices, policy-suppression disclosure) is proposed as binding client conformance (**LP-8**), plus: a realm change is a chrome-owned transition, and guest→account promotion never starts from page content. The **guest ladder G0–G3** lands the Ideas guest-deep-link requirement; the recommended default is **G1** — client-verified authorship/bytes over a hosted endpoint, absence *never claimed* (**LP-5**).

**LN-9 — Seams 8 and 12 closed; the channel is a KEL citizen.** A lens channel **reuses KEL control and recovery**: channel epoch = the admission receipt's `authEpoch`; control epochs, channel recovery verifiers, and second guardian roots are deleted; `ADOPT` (the fork-clearing transition — the channel's only security transition) joins `RESET` in the **control-only, non-grantable** class after the red team stole it with a scoped grant, and any transition out of `CHANNEL_CONTESTED` is a loud subscriber ceremony regardless of issuer, never an ordinary diff. Personal-vs-published separation (PP-1…PP-6) is normative: personal instances local/encrypted with structurally unserializable `PRIVATE_HANDLE` refs; the routine freshness check runs against **your own node/replica by default** (the batched remote check ships your entire roster to the endpoint on every focus — a better identifier than a cookie); the honest limit stated plainly: a content-addressed public system cannot prevent *confirmation* of a correctly-guessed policy (**LP-7**).

**LN-10 — Scale stays honest, and the growth vectors are fenced.** The 15–55 center held under attack; what breaks past it is gate composability first, latency second, legibility third — compilation does not. Two fences the red team forced: the client counts **effective authority principals across all saved Views** (one-tap "see it as they curate it" was silent web-of-trust by attrition — LP-4 rider), and **computed membership** (`derivationKind: CURATED | COMPUTED`) is pin-only, never channel-followed, with per-adoption ceremonies (**LP-10** — the guardrail James's own achievement idea needs, with Nostr's WoT field failure as evidence). Kernel-counter ruling: `viewMutationVersion(author)` is the MUST-grade E2 candidate; `positionSeq` is demoted to an option (two of its three consumers dissolved); the claimant roster stays lean-adopt with LR-3's corrected framing. One joint E2 row for all three — never three competing asks.
