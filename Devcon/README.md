## Submitted application

- **Title:** Who Can Turn Off Your Ethereum App? A Full-Stack Walk-Away Test
- **Track:** Permissionless Networks
- **Format:** Talk — 20 minutes plus 5 minutes Q&A
- **Tags, in order:** Censorship Resistance; Decentralization; Open Source
- **Thesis:** a content hash or decentralized carrier does not by itself make an
  application durable. Teach a layer-by-layer walk-away test for operator-owned
  dependencies, using live EFS on Sepolia and its independently reproducible
  proof as the case study.
- **Positioning rule:** EFS is the evidence, not the product being promoted.
