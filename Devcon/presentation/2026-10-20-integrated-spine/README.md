The story starts with a person keeping years of work, makes developers the
people who can protect that freedom, and ends with shared foundations others
can build on. Eight functional jobs remain grouped into entering the app,
continuing the work and keeping the rules. Absence and hostile authority stay
distinct. User-chosen software and shared-rule changes now have an explicit
comparison.
