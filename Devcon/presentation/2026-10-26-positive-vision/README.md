# Positive vision and practical walk-away checks

Saved October 26, 2026 after James asked for positive teaching, a clearer
hyperstructure vision, shared-data examples, immutable contracts and a
concrete closing checklist. The accepted title and the official Devcon theme
remain. This is the native rehearsal copy, not an organizer submission.

## What changed

- The introduction shows why lasting software matters to people and builders.
  A new diagram explains shared protocols that people can use without their
  creators and that different applications can build on.
- The three chapters stay Interface, Data and Rules. Eight functional jobs
  organize the stack without making the audience learn a scoring system.
- EFS is mainly in the data chapter, beside EAS and Nostr. Damus and Amethyst
  illustrate two clients using a shared social identity, public posts and
  follows. They are separate examples, not a claimed integration.
- Editable diagrams explain DNS/HTTP versus ENS/IPFS, shared social records,
  and checking an EFS file reference against alternative copies.
- A dedicated immutable-contract slide encourages a stable core, with
  Uniswap v2 as the example and voluntary migration to new deployments.
- The closing checklist gives four user checks and four operator checks,
  followed by one action: try an important task without the team and improve
  one dependency.
- Technical qualifications and sources are in optional speaker notes. The
  main narration explains useful improvements and checks in plain language.
